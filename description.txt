Soulmates 0.24.0 - Learning Together | Beta Candidate

Create personal companions with the reusable Soulcore. Every Soulbound Sigil stores one companion's appearance, personality, talent, bond, mood, energy, level, learned habits, cargo and memories. Keep the active Sigil in your inventory. A companion named AETHER has all talents and learned perks.

Existing actions learn bounded contextual preferences from observations, requests, outcomes and evaluations. Successful work may ask "Was that helpful?" in the existing answer wheel. Approval, another idea and less of this guide later selection; Later, silence and cancellation are neutral. Quiet disables evaluations. Permissions and existing work rules retain priority. Construction-item use provides context, not placement proof or free building. Learning persists per Sigil; pending evaluations do not.

Terraria remains the default mouse mode: native interactions have priority. Right-click your companion for her Soulwheel, or yourself for Player tools and Emotes. Unhandled clicks on nearby NPCs, loose drops and natural resources open their target context. Empty air, furniture and placed torches do not open fallback wheels. Me and Soulmate arm the next world right-click; the selected target stays fixed while choosing. Right-click inside a wheel cycles modes; X or Escape restores normal use. Inventory, typing, NPC dialogue, alternate item actions and cursor-held items retain priority.

Companions defend, heal by talent, retrieve real drops, mine safe materials and investigate treasure. Work directly offers Find nearby ore and Mine area beside Config and Last. Config unfolds area/pointed work, mining modes and automatic filters; Last stores the last accepted explicit recipe per Sigil. Pointed work selects a fresh target, never old coordinates or item slots. Failed orders, Look and settings do not replace Last. Tunnel offers Up/Right/Down/Left/Auto, Short (up to eight steps) or Until opening (up to 24, bounded by work radius). Cardinal routes stop at liquid, unstable material, protected tiles and decorations, also after planning. Config creates no work; changes during area mining replan it. Automatic terrain needs opt-in; direct orders preserve plant protection, tool strength and progression. Forester/AETHER shakes supported trees, clears fallen logs and replants real acorns. A carried axe allows verified dry side-twig pruning; living/unsupported parts remain protected.

Commands > Initiative Rules sets Ask / Always / Never independently for gathering, mining, forestry, treasure, critter company and catching. Answer work-permission prompts with Yes, No, Always or Never. Yes approves one target, not future work. Reset stops automatic work and restores Ask. Pause retains work, Resume continues it, Abort clears it and holds work until resumed or replaced. Defense still works. The shared activity controller prioritizes defense, pause, assignments, critters, automatic work, resident visits, Stay, replies and following; interrupted assignments retain their state.

Following restores energy gradually; working also recovers a little. Light pickup costs one energy per eight retrieved stacks, mining one per four blocks and forestry one per two successful actions. Exhausted work rebuilds a 30-energy reserve before resuming. Combat does not grant work recovery. Care > Rest is optional.

Critters offers Watch, Company, Catch Critters and Off, with follower count and permission/paused/busy/manual feedback. Watch greets without catching; creature/gesture replies wait behind speech and questions. Company invites one real critter, or three for AETHER. Native physics and despawning remain; small obstacles allow hops, never noclip or teleporting. Explicit visits and invited followers work with autonomy off, without changing automatic consent. Pause stops guided movement; Off/recall releases followers. Catch Critters has a basic net capability, not a withdrawable item; lava critters require a carried lava-proof net. Native catching and cargo limits apply; gold, statue-spawned and released animals remain protected. Automatic Company/Collect need separate consent, autonomy and stamina. Witnessed deaths can prompt queued grief or disapproval; catches are not deaths. AETHER's cosmetic flock represents real carried insects.

Pets opens from her wheel and character panel. Give reusable pet items from your inventory; favorites stay with you. Twelve owned slots hold real items. Giving the first item selects it automatically. Left-click an owned pet to summon; right-click to take back. Status distinguishes no summon, waiting and an active familiar. Additional gifts do not replace an active pet or override dismissal. Critters retains paged choices. One harmless familiar follows her without consuming its item or changing your pet/buffs. Dismiss, final withdrawal, recall and switching remove it. Native frames use custom floating movement, not original pet AI. Light pets give basic light; special utilities, multipart art and arbitrary modded drawing are not copied. Existing cargo remains; all peers must use 0.24.0.

Details opens Conversation, Equipment, Diagnostics and Pets. The Sigil reports its actual shortcut, not a guaranteed V. Portrait, vitals, native tools and cargo accompany a separate scrollable reply. Equipment exposes capabilities, not armor. Diagnostics shows activity, permissions, learning, stamina, critter/pet state and input gates. Export writes SoulmatesFeedback/diagnostic-latest.txt locally without recording/uploading. MP labels client state, not server history.

Town visits use native emotes and personality-aware replies. Talk > Items discusses cargo by category; "Do you remember?" recalls shared events. Games contains fair Rock, paper, scissors without XP. Bond groups Feelings, Gestures and Care. Player Emotes sorts all 151 native symbols into categories. Back moves one level; arrows page. All six automatic abilities share attention; questions do not block approved Always visits. Ask/Never still apply.

Personal questions offer four native-symbol responses affecting mood, bond and tone without approving unrelated work. Quiet/Calm/Chatty controls personal questions, not work-permission prompts. Approved automatic work can continue during questions. Wallet gifts offer personality-specific "Here for you!" lines and four responses. Expression symbols last three seconds. Speech stays near her, fades without restarting on repeated lines and remains visible for pending questions. Corner-minimap avoidance uses available space.

Equipment, Resources and Wallet are separate. Resources have 60 stack slots and a per-type reserve of 50 units per level: 500 at level 10, 1,000 at level 20. Native stack limits still apply. The wallet has no amount or level cap. Changed multiplayer inventory slots reject a transfer rather than being overwritten. Existing Sigils retain their identity and saved permissions; unidentified legacy modded material knowledge must be relearned. Full cargo is reported, not silently discarded.

All nine Terraria 1.4.4 languages are included. New translations are AI-authored and welcome native-speaker feedback; some multiplayer replies still use the server language. Learning is local game logic, not an online AI service. Optional AETHER Field Notes and the in-game Mailbox never upload data. Automatic records omit names, chat and exact positions; typed notes are saved separately and retained on failed writing.

Beta candidate, not completed stability certification. Rendered controls, critter reactions, long sessions, progression and connected MP need repeatable tests. Use the same version on all peers. Report the action, expected/actual result and solo/MP. Local notes are not sent to the developer. Workshop/mod icons and Soulkin art are AI-generated; other named native visuals reuse Terraria assets.
