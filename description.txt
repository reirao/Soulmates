Soulmates 0.19.3 - Steady Hands | Alpha

Create personal companions with the reusable Soulcore. Each Soulbound Sigil saves one companion's appearance, personality, growth, learned habits, cargo, and memories. A companion named AETHER has every talent and learned perk. Showing a nearby drop or tile invites a little shared observation: personality and chosen voice color the reply, while Direct stays factual. Inspection itself changes no assignment, cargo, terrain, permissions or experience.

Terraria is the default mouse mode. Native interactions run first; unhandled clicks on nearby NPCs, loose drops and natural resources open their target context directly. Empty air and furniture do not open fallback wheels. Right-click the companion for her Soulwheel or yourself for Player tools and Emotes. Selecting Me or Soulmate closes the wheel and arms the next world right-click. The clicked target stays selected while choosing an action. Right-click in an open wheel cycles modes; X or Escape restores normal use. Inventory, typing, native NPC dialogue, alternate item actions and cursor-held items retain priority.

Nearby work is weighted by talent, distance, learned habits, and waiting time. Choose Ask / Always / Never separately for each initiative. Answer wheels open at the player while she shows the task symbol and a question mark. She defends, heals, gathers, mines safe reachable materials, shakes trees, clears fallen logs, and replants carried acorns with the Forester perk. A real axe allows pruning dry vanilla side branches; automatic pruning needs Forester or AETHER. Leafy branches, stems, ground and unsupported tree families remain protected. Commands > Pause keeps the assignment, Resume continues it, and Abort clears it and holds automatic work until resumed or replaced. Defense remains available.

Town visits include native NPC emotes and personality-aware replies. Saved resident relationships are scoped by world, NPC type, and name; she remembers new acquaintances and friends. Talk Mode > Bond can recall them. "Do you remember?" tells short chapters from actual shared events. Items offers native-symbol categories for food, ores, tools, weapons, recovery, materials and critters, describing actual cargo or a held example. Long replies can be scrolled. Occasional comments reflect visible creatures, drops, weather and the current situation, without interrupting speech. Companion wheel > Bond > Rock, paper, scissors opens a fair little game with personality-aware reactions and no XP rewards.

Player Emotes > Items opens Food, Recovery, Tools, Weapons, Materials, Valuables and Party groups before the native symbols. All 151 vanilla emotes remain available. Ore conversations remain in Companion wheel > Items > Ores; Terraria has no dedicated ore-family emote symbols in this wheel.

Companion wheel > Work > Mining approach > Automatic mining selects allowed ore and observed-block types with native item icons and paging. Ores start enabled; observed terrain needs explicit opt-in. Selections persist on the Sigil. These rules affect autonomous mining, not explicit assignments. Every mining path rechecks nearby plants, saplings, trees and decorations before removing support blocks. Tool strength and the chosen mining approach remain required.

Light pickup costs one energy per eight retrieved stacks, mining one per four blocks, and forestry one per two successful actions. Following restores two energy every two seconds; working restores one every three seconds. Exhausted work rebuilds a 30-energy reserve before automatically resuming. Combat retains priority and grants no work recovery; healing costs two energy. Approved opportunities have shorter revisit delays without overriding Ask/Never rules.

The Critters branch offers Watch, Company, Pet Collect and Off. All enabled critter modes can greet natural creatures during work; speech waits behind existing lines and is discarded when the creature leaves. Between completed automatic tasks, bounded visits get a turn before more automatic work. Running work, defense, Stay, prompts, low energy and disabled autonomy retain priority. Right-click context offers Look, Company and Collect for the selected eligible creature. Direct visits replace the old assignment. Collect requires a real carried net and cargo room; lava-net rules apply. Gold, statue-spawned and released critters remain protected. Company guides real critters: one normally, up to three for AETHER. Native physics, catches and despawning remain; Off or recall releases them without copies or teleporting. Visible deaths prompt grief or disapproval based on recorded attack credit; catching is not dying. Reactions give no XP or penalties. Soul Bolts leave harmless creatures alone. AETHER's cosmetic carried-insect flock remains; vanilla vanity pets stay player-bound.

Equipment, Resources, and Wallet remain separate. Resources have 60 stack slots and a per-type limit of 50 units per level: 500 at level 10 and 1,000 at level 20. Collected coins use an uncapped wallet. Existing Sigils remain compatible. Keep the active Sigil in your inventory; moving it out recalls the companion. Full cargo is reported explicitly.

Personal questions offer four native-symbol responses: keep collecting nearby drops, be playful, give gentle company, or talk later. They affect mood, bond, and saved conversation tone without approving unrelated mining or forestry. Approved nearby drops are collected during Follow without another long trip. The wallet occasionally offers new savings with personality-specific "Here for you!" lines. Accept, save together, respond playfully, or defer; coins that cannot fit stay safe with the companion. The wallet itself has no cap.

All nine Terraria 1.4.4 languages: English, German, Italian, French, Spanish, Russian, Brazilian Portuguese, Polish, Simplified Chinese. Follows the game's language. New translations are AI-authored; native-speaker feedback is welcome. Some multiplayer replies still use the server language. Learning is local game logic, not an online AI service. Optional AETHER Field Notes and the in-game feedback mailbox stay on this computer and never upload data. Automatic records omit names, chat, and exact positions; typed notes retain what you write. Growing alpha: full progression, long sessions, and live multiplayer still need testing.
