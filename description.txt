Soulmates 0.7.4: Create a companion with a soul.

Use the reusable Soulcore to shape unique companions, bind each one to a Soulbound Sigil, and bring them along on your Terraria adventures.

Create persistent animated companions with personality, bond ranks, memories, autonomous combat or healing roles, equipment, their own safely synchronized inventory, useful jobs, and a light that reveals the places they explore. Existing Sigils remain compatible, and switching always leaves exactly one active companion.
