Soulmates 0.20.0 - Shared Instincts | Beta Candidate

Create personal companions with the reusable Soulcore. Every Soulbound Sigil stores one companion's appearance, personality, talent, bond, mood, energy, level, learned habits, cargo and memories. Keep the active Sigil in your inventory. A companion named AETHER has all talents and learned perks.

Terraria remains the default mouse mode: native interactions have priority. Right-click your companion for her Soulwheel, or yourself for Player tools and Emotes. Unhandled clicks on nearby NPCs, loose drops and natural resources open their target context. Empty air, furniture and placed torches do not open fallback wheels. Me and Soulmate arm the next world right-click; the selected target stays fixed while choosing. Right-click inside a wheel cycles modes; X or Escape restores normal use. Inventory, typing, NPC dialogue, alternate item actions and cursor-held items retain priority.

Companions defend nearby threats, heal, collect real drops, mine reachable materials, investigate chests and learn from observed player activities. Forester/AETHER can shake supported trees, clear fallen logs and replant carried acorns. A carried axe allows safe pruning of verified dry vanilla side twigs. Leafy branches, trunks, saplings, decorations and their support blocks remain protected. Native tool strength and progression rules apply. Work > Mining approach offers Adaptive, Tunnel, Vein and Surface. Automatic mining uses paged native icons to enable individual ores and observed materials; terrain needs opt-in. Direct orders do not bypass safety or tool requirements.

Commands > Initiative Rules sets Ask / Always / Never independently for gathering, mining, forestry, treasure, critter company and collecting. Answer task > question-mark prompts with Yes, No, Always or Never. Yes approves one target only. Direct target orders approve that visit, not future work. Reset stops automatic work and restores Ask. Pause retains the current assignment, Resume continues it, and Abort clears it and holds work until resumed or replaced. Defense still works. The shared activity controller prioritizes defense, pause, assignments, critters, automatic work, resident visits, Stay, replies and following; interrupted assignments retain their state. These priorities govern task movement, not every passive observation or permitted nearby pickup.

Following restores energy gradually; working also recovers a little. Light pickup costs one energy per eight retrieved stacks, mining one per four blocks and forestry one per two successful actions. Exhausted work rebuilds a 30-energy reserve before resuming. Combat does not grant work recovery. Care > Rest is optional.

Critters offers Watch, Company, Pet Collect and Off. Watch notices creatures without moving or catching. Greetings pair creature > greeting symbols with a native critter reply. Notices wait behind speech and questions; departed creatures receive no stale reply. Company guides one real critter, or three for AETHER. Native physics, damage and despawning remain; Off or recall releases them without copies or teleporting. Collect includes a basic Bug Net capability, not a withdrawable item. Lava critters need a carried lava-proof net. Native catches and cargo limits apply; gold, statue-spawned and player-released creatures remain protected. Company and Collect have separate consent rules, defaulting to Ask on existing Sigils. Visible deaths can prompt grief or disapproval without XP or penalties. AETHER's cosmetic flock represents real carried insects. Extra vanity pets remain player-bound.

Town visits use native emotes and personality-aware replies; relationships remember world and resident identity. Talk > Items offers Food, Ores, Tools, Weapons, Recovery, Materials and Critters, based on real cargo or a held example. "Do you remember?" recalls shared events instead of inventing a past. Long replies scroll. Companion wheel > Games > Rock, paper, scissors uses independent choices, fair outcomes and native move symbols; no XP rewards. Player Emotes includes all 151 vanilla symbols, with item subcategories.

Personal questions offer four native-symbol responses affecting mood, bond and tone without approving unrelated work. Commands > Personal questions chooses Quiet, Calm or Chatty; Quiet does not suppress work-permission prompts. Approved automatic work can continue during personal questions. Wallet gifts use personality-specific "Here for you!" lines; accept, keep saving, respond playfully or defer. Every expression symbol lasts three seconds. Speech keeps its fade and companion anchor instead of restarting on repeated lines.

Equipment, Resources and Wallet are separate. Resources have 60 stack slots and a per-type reserve of 50 units per level: 500 at level 10, 1,000 at level 20. Native stack limits still apply. The wallet has no amount or level cap. Changed multiplayer inventory slots reject a transfer rather than being overwritten. Existing Sigils retain their identity and saved permissions; unidentified legacy modded material knowledge must be relearned. Full cargo is reported, not silently discarded.

All nine Terraria 1.4.4 languages are included. New translations are AI-authored and welcome native-speaker feedback; some multiplayer replies still use the server language. Learning is local game logic, not an online AI service. Optional AETHER Field Notes and the in-game Mailbox never upload data. Automatic records omit names, chat and exact positions; typed notes are saved separately and retained on failed writing.

Published beta candidate, not a completed stability certification. The creator's first playtest broadly works; controls, critter reactions, long sessions, full progression and connected multiplayer still need repeatable tests. Features are frozen during stabilization. All multiplayer peers must use 0.20.0. Please report the action, expected result, actual result and solo/MP. Local mailbox notes are not automatically sent to the developer. Workshop icon, mod icon and custom Soulkin art are AI-generated; other named native visuals reuse Terraria assets.
