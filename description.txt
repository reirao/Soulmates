Soulmates 0.22.1 - Showcase Repairs | Beta Candidate

Create personal companions with the reusable Soulcore. Every Soulbound Sigil stores one companion's appearance, personality, talent, bond, mood, energy, level, learned habits, cargo and memories. Keep the active Sigil in your inventory. A companion named AETHER has all talents and learned perks.

Terraria remains the default mouse mode: native interactions have priority. Right-click your companion for her Soulwheel, or yourself for Player tools and Emotes. Unhandled clicks on nearby NPCs, loose drops and natural resources open their target context. Empty air, furniture and placed torches do not open fallback wheels. Me and Soulmate arm the next world right-click; the selected target stays fixed while choosing. Right-click inside a wheel cycles modes; X or Escape restores normal use. Inventory, typing, NPC dialogue, alternate item actions and cursor-held items retain priority.

Companions defend, heal by talent, retrieve real drops, mine safe materials and investigate treasure. Work opens Config and Last. Config unfolds existing area/pointed work, mining modes and automatic filters; Last stores the last accepted explicit recipe per Sigil. Area mining restores its saved approach/direction/stop mode. Pointed work selects a fresh target, never old coordinates or item slots. Failed orders, Look and settings do not replace Last. Tunnel offers Up/Right/Down/Left or Auto, Short (up to eight steps) or Until opening (up to 24, reduced by work radius). Cardinal routes stop at liquid, unstable material, protected tiles and decorations, also after planning. Config creates no work; changes during area mining replan it. Automatic terrain needs opt-in; direct orders never bypass plant protection, tool strength or progression. Forester/AETHER shakes supported trees, clears fallen logs and replants real acorns. A carried axe allows verified dry side-twig pruning; living/unsupported parts remain protected.

Commands > Initiative Rules sets Ask / Always / Never independently for gathering, mining, forestry, treasure, critter company and collecting. Answer task > question-mark prompts with Yes, No, Always or Never. Yes approves one target only. Direct target orders approve that visit, not future work. Reset stops automatic work and restores Ask. Pause retains the current assignment, Resume continues it, and Abort clears it and holds work until resumed or replaced. Defense still works. The shared activity controller prioritizes defense, pause, assignments, critters, automatic work, resident visits, Stay, replies and following; interrupted assignments retain their state. These priorities govern task movement, not every passive observation or permitted nearby pickup.

Following restores energy gradually; working also recovers a little. Light pickup costs one energy per eight retrieved stacks, mining one per four blocks and forestry one per two successful actions. Exhausted work rebuilds a 30-energy reserve before resuming. Combat does not grant work recovery. Care > Rest is optional.

Critters offers Watch, Company, Catch Critters and Off, with follower count and permission/paused/busy/manual state feedback. Watch greets without catching; native creature/gesture replies wait behind speech and questions. Company invites one real critter, or three for AETHER. Native physics and despawning remain; small ground obstacles allow hops, never noclip or teleporting. Invited animals keep following with autonomy off; Pause stops guided movement. Explicit pointed Company/Collect also work with autonomy off, without changing automatic consent. Off/recall releases followers. Catch Critters has a basic net capability, not a withdrawable item; lava critters require a carried lava-proof net. Native catching and cargo limits apply; gold, statue-spawned and released creatures remain protected. Automatic Company/Collect still require separate consent, autonomy and stamina. Witnessed deaths can prompt queued grief or disapproval; catches are not deaths. AETHER's cosmetic flock represents real carried insects. Companion pet selects a real carried Zephyr Fish/Nectar item for one harmless animated follower. The item remains in cargo; dismiss, last-item withdrawal, recall and companion switching remove the follower. Your own pet and buffs stay unchanged. Only Zephyr Fish/Baby Hornet are supported, using native sprite frames with custom companion-following movement. Appearance, real critters and the insect flock remain separate.

Details or V opens the character window: Conversation, Equipment and Diagnostics. A larger portrait and vitals sit beside native tool/trinket displays, pet choices and cargo; her scrollable reply has its own bottom strip. Equipment exposes carried tools/capabilities, not generic armor slots. Pause, Resume and Abort are available in every view. Diagnostics shows live activity, permissions, stamina, critter eligibility and owned pets. Export writes only SoulmatesFeedback/diagnostic-latest.txt locally; it enables no recording and uploads nothing. In MP it labels client state, not authoritative server history.

Town visits use native emotes and personality-aware replies. Talk > Items discusses real cargo by category; "Do you remember?" recalls shared events. Games contains fair Rock, paper, scissors, without XP rewards. Bond groups Feelings, Gestures and Care. Player Emotes sorts all 151 vanilla symbols into semantic categories and subcategories. Back moves one level; arrows page within a group. Critter Company/Collect now share attention scheduling with gathering, mining, forestry and treasure; personal questions do not block already approved Always visits. Ask/Never still apply.

Personal questions offer four native-symbol responses affecting mood, bond and tone without approving unrelated work. Commands > Personal questions chooses Quiet, Calm or Chatty; Quiet does not suppress work-permission prompts. Approved automatic work can continue during personal questions. Wallet gifts use personality-specific "Here for you!" lines; accept, keep saving, respond playfully or defer. Every expression symbol lasts three seconds. Speech keeps its fade and companion anchor instead of restarting on repeated lines; unanswered questions hold the fade until resolved or expired. Corner minimap avoidance uses available viewport space.

Equipment, Resources and Wallet are separate. Resources have 60 stack slots and a per-type reserve of 50 units per level: 500 at level 10, 1,000 at level 20. Native stack limits still apply. The wallet has no amount or level cap. Changed multiplayer inventory slots reject a transfer rather than being overwritten. Existing Sigils retain their identity and saved permissions; unidentified legacy modded material knowledge must be relearned. Full cargo is reported, not silently discarded.

All nine Terraria 1.4.4 languages are included. New translations are AI-authored and welcome native-speaker feedback; some multiplayer replies still use the server language. Learning is local game logic, not an online AI service. Optional AETHER Field Notes and the in-game Mailbox never upload data. Automatic records omit names, chat and exact positions; typed notes are saved separately and retained on failed writing.

Beta candidate, not completed stability certification. Rendered controls, critter reactions, long sessions, progression and connected MP need repeatable tests. Use the same version on all peers. Report the action, expected/actual result and solo/MP. Local notes are not sent to the developer. Workshop/mod icons and Soulkin art are AI-generated; other named native visuals reuse Terraria assets.
