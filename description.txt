Soulmates 0.27.0 - Behind the Curtain | Beta

Create personal companions with the reusable Soulcore. Each Soulbound Sigil carries one companion's appearance, personality, talent, mood, energy, bond, level, learned preferences, cargo, pets and memories. Keep the active Sigil in your inventory. A companion named AETHER has all talents and learned perks. One companion per player; switching retires old effects.

YOUR COMPANION CAN BECOME THE BOSS
The same companion, with the same memories and inventory. EVIL's angry face is directly on her wheel. Turn it on, open Games and click the secret eye, then confirm a real three-phase duel. Your character can take real damage. Stop/Abort, EVIL off or recall ends it; work stays paused until Resume. Details below explain the safeguards.

SMALL WHEELS, CLEAR INTENTIONS
Right-click her for companion tools; yourself for player tools and categorized native Emotes. Terraria is the default mouse mode and retains priority over ordinary interactions. Mining offers Adaptive, Tunnel, Vein and Surface, automatic ore/material filters, compass directions and bounded passage digging. HER OWN LITTLE COMPANY
Pets has a staff-symbol toggle for a separate Retinue inventory. Give real unfavorited reusable summon staves; left-click an owned staff to call company and right-click to return it. Native projectile artwork uses bounded companion melee/ranged/sentry combat, not original player-minion AI or arbitrary modded drawing. Your buffs, pet, minion and sentry slots stay separate. Recall, switching, dismissal and final withdrawal retire bound effects.

A PLACE TO BELONG
Shelter and interesting loot have different meanings. A chosen bed, assigned NPC housing and familiar sheltered places provide different evidence. Repeated returns can lead to a question in the existing answer wheel: our home, her little home, just a stop, or later. Only your answer establishes shared or companion ownership. Bounded place memory is per Sigil and world-scoped; changed shelter is rechecked. Recognized home storage is not automatically announced as unexplored treasure; explicit treasure search remains available. She does not construct or remodel houses.

Home on her wheel opens those four choices directly, even at Stay, with autonomy off or Quiet questions. Explicit requests need no repeated visits and can reclassify an existing home. Ongoing work and resident housing are respected.

EVIL™
Off by default; toggle the angry face directly on her wheel, Commands or the character panel. Normal EVIL is theatrical expression, not destructive permission. With EVIL on, Games reveals a secret eye: click once, then confirm to challenge her as a real three-phase boss. Two-second warmup, visible HP, telegraphed dashes and fan attacks. Your character can take real damage. No loot/XP, terrain edits or permanent companion death. Abort, Games stop, EVIL off or recall ends it; work stays paused until Resume. Inventory and memories survive. Other players and critters are not targets.

CARGO AND CHARACTER
Open-ended exact XP and levels; no gameplay level cap. Equipment: 8-12 slots, 99 units per item type. Resources: 60 slots, 50 units per level/type, up to 1,000 at level 20, respecting native stack limits. Further levels preserve that capacity and ordinary retinue balance. Wallet: no gameplay currency cap. Transfers preserve actual items. Details opens conversation, equipment, cargo, pets and read-only diagnostics. The Sigil displays its actual Details binding; V remains unverified in real play.

Nine languages follow Terraria: EN, DE, IT, FR, ES, RU, PT-BR, PL and Simplified Chinese. New translations are AI-authored; corrections welcome. Some multiplayer replies use the server language.
