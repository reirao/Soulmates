Soulmates 0.10.0 - AETHER-EGG Alpha: Shape a companion that watches, learns, and grows beside you.

Use the reusable Soulcore to shape unique companions, including the Omni Soul AETHER, bind each one to a Soulbound Sigil, and bring them along on your Terraria adventures.

Persistent animated companions learn five practical instincts from your behavior, defend and heal, gather and mine, tend forests, carry a synchronized inventory, and recover naturally. A dedicated Terraria-emote prompt lets you answer Yes, No, Always, or Never when they discover an opportunity. The icon-based Soulwheel combines commands, work, bonding, every vanilla Terraria emote, direct pack access, and details. Existing Sigils remain compatible.
