Soulmates 0.17.1 - Shared Discoveries | Alpha

Create personal companions with the reusable Soulcore. Each Soulbound Sigil saves one companion's appearance, personality, growth, learned habits, cargo, and memories. A companion named AETHER has every talent and learned perk. Showing a nearby drop or tile invites a little shared observation: personality and chosen voice color the reply, while Direct stays factual. Inspection itself changes no assignment, cargo, terrain, permissions or experience.

Terraria is the default mouse mode for normal world and item use. Right-click the companion for her Soulwheel or yourself for Player tools and Emotes. Native-symbol selectors choose Terraria, Me, or Soulmate. Me points out what the cursor sees; Soulmate offers suitable Look, Gather, Mine, and Forest tasks for the original clicked target. Right-click in an open wheel cycles modes; X or Escape restores normal use. Inventory, text entry, NPC dialogue and cursor-held items retain priority.

Nearby work is weighted by talent, distance, learned habits, and waiting time. Choose Ask / Always / Never separately for each initiative. Answer wheels open at the player while she shows the task symbol and a question mark. She defends, heals, gathers, mines safe reachable materials, shakes trees, clears fallen logs, and replants carried acorns with the Forester perk. Living tree-branch pruning is not enabled.

Town visits include native NPC emotes and personality-aware replies. Saved resident relationships are scoped by world, NPC type, and name; she remembers new acquaintances and friends. Talk Mode > Bond can recall them. Her richer symbol responses include comfort and disagreement instead of simply copying your emote.

The Critters branch offers Watch, Company, Pet Collect and Off. Watch notices small creatures with quiet personality-specific replies and native emotes. Pet Collect needs a carried bug net and free cargo; native lava-net requirements still apply. Gold, statue-spawned and player-released critters are left alone. Company gently guides existing natural bunnies, squirrels, birds, butterflies, fireflies and lightning bugs: one for an ordinary companion, up to three for AETHER. They remain real, temporary world NPCs with normal collision, health, catching and despawning; Off or recall releases them. No pets are spawned or teleported through terrain. AETHER's item-backed cosmetic insect flock remains available. Vanilla vanity pets still belong to players.

Equipment, Resources, and Wallet remain separate. Resources have 60 stack slots and a per-type limit of 50 units per level: 500 at level 10 and 1,000 at level 20. Collected coins use an uncapped wallet. Existing Sigils remain compatible. Keep the active Sigil in your inventory; moving it out recalls the companion. Full cargo is reported explicitly.

Personal questions offer four native-symbol responses: keep collecting nearby drops, be playful, give gentle company, or talk later. They affect mood, bond, and saved conversation tone without approving unrelated mining or forestry. Helpful work takes priority over critter visits; approved nearby drops are collected during Follow without another long trip. The wallet occasionally offers new savings with personality-specific "Here for you!" lines. Accept, save together, respond playfully, or defer; coins that cannot fit stay safe with the companion. The wallet itself has no cap.

English and German. Learning is local game logic, not an online AI service. Optional AETHER Field Notes and the in-game feedback mailbox stay on this computer and never upload data. Automatic records omit names, chat, and exact positions; typed notes retain what you write. Growing alpha: full progression, long sessions, and live multiplayer still need testing.
