Soulmates 0.28.1 - A Little Flock | Beta

Create personal companions with the reusable Soulcore. Each Soulbound Sigil carries one companion's appearance, personality, talent, mood, energy, bond, level, learned preferences, cargo, pets and memories. Keep the active Sigil in your inventory. A companion named AETHER has all talents and learned perks. One companion per player; switching retires old effects.

SMALL WHEELS, CLEAR INTENTIONS
Right-click her for companion tools; yourself for player tools and categorized native Emotes. Terraria is the default mouse mode and retains priority over ordinary interactions. Me and Soulmate offer explicit contextual pointing and orders. Work includes ore search, area mining, Config and Last. Mining offers Adaptive, Tunnel, Vein and Surface, automatic ore/material filters, compass directions and bounded passage digging. Tool progression, plants, saplings, decorations and liquid protection still apply. Pause keeps a task, Resume continues and Abort cancels it.

LEARNING TOGETHER
Existing abilities use separate Ask/Always/Never permissions. Observations, accepted requests, actual outcomes and occasional evaluations shape bounded per-Sigil preferences. Approval, another idea and less of this guide later choices; Later, silence and cancellation are neutral. Quiet disables automatic personal questions. Local game logic, not online AI, free-building or a replacement for explicit commands.

SMALL PERSONAL UNDERTAKINGS
During safe idle time she may revisit a classified home, keep her familiar company or reflect on an observed building supply. Return precedes a completed memory; existing answers shape preferences and stories. Work, danger, Stay, Pause and consent retain priority. No construction or remodeling.

HER OWN LITTLE COMPANY
Pets in her wheel or Details stacks matching vanilla pet items, including Eucalyptus Sap: twelve stack slots, 64 total pet items. Each unit calls one harmless familiar in a separated formation. Select an owned item to call all with that type leading; Dismiss rests all. Right-click returns one item. First explicit gift selects only if empty; later gifts respect dismissal. Gifts and pickup share capacity; overflow stays with its source. Player inventory limits stay native; modded pets keep their stack limits. Native artwork and animation use companion movement, not original player-pet AI or arbitrary modded drawing.

Pets has a staff-symbol toggle for a separate Retinue inventory. Give real unfavorited reusable summon staves; left-click an owned staff to call company and right-click to return it. First explicit gift selects only when storage is empty; later gifts respect dismissal. Normal companions select 1/2/3 distinct staff types at levels 1/10/20; AETHER selects all distinct owned types, within 64 item slots. Duplicates do not multiply helpers. Native projectile artwork uses bounded companion melee/ranged/sentry combat, not original player-minion AI or arbitrary modded drawing. Your buffs, pet, minion and sentry slots stay separate. Recall, switching, dismissal and final withdrawal retire bound effects.

A PLACE TO BELONG
Shelter and interesting loot have different meanings. A chosen bed, assigned NPC housing and familiar sheltered places provide different evidence. Repeated returns can lead to a question in the existing answer wheel: our home, her little home, just a stop, or later. Only your answer establishes shared or companion ownership. Bounded place memory is per Sigil and world-scoped; changed shelter is rechecked. Recognized home storage is not automatically announced as unexplored treasure; explicit treasure search remains available. She does not construct or remodel houses.

Home on her wheel opens these choices directly, including at Stay or with autonomy off.

EVIL™
Off by default; toggle the angry face directly on her wheel, Commands or the character panel. Normal EVIL is theatrical expression, not destructive permission. With EVIL on, Games reveals a secret eye: click once, then confirm to challenge her as a real three-phase boss. Two-second warmup, visible HP, telegraphed dashes and fan attacks. Your character can take real damage. No loot/XP, terrain edits or permanent companion death. Abort, Games stop, EVIL off or recall ends it; work stays paused until Resume. Inventory and memories survive. Other players and critters are not targets.

USEFUL COMPANY
Defense, talent-based healing, real drop collection, safe mining, forest tending and treasure awareness. Low energy and mood recover naturally. Real threats take priority over training dummies. Jobs validate current targets and bounded timeouts. Stale selections and replies cannot silently command another companion.

CAREFUL FINDS
Salvage in a find's context wheel asks for only this object or captured matching finds nearby. A second confirmation covers unknown origins, up to 16 matches within 12 tiles. Gems, pots, cobwebs and selected coral, books, bottles, candles, statues, banners and paintings are supported, not arbitrary furniture. Observed construction, recognized homes, beds, housing, wiring, paint, unsafe supports and protected objects block removal. Old objects have unknown provenance, not proven world ownership. Native destruction and normal pickup preserve real drops; Pause/Resume/Abort remain available.

CRITTERS AND PETS
Meet and nearby encounters offer try making friends, catch this one, watch or leave. Collect Always may find an encounter but still asks before catching. Company invites supported wild species, including both scorpions, using bounded native movement. Collect has built-in ordinary net capability; lava species need a carried eligible net. Gold, statue-spawned and released critters are protected. A catch becomes a real Pack item, not a follower; queued receipts name it and its destination. Temporary Company and existing species memory are not persistent individual-animal relationships. Catching and death remain distinct. Permissions, visibility, tasks and recovery still matter.

CARGO AND CHARACTER
Open-ended exact XP and levels; no gameplay level cap. Equipment: 8-12 slots, 99 units per item type. Resources: 60 slots, 50 units per level/type, up to 1,000 at level 20, respecting native stack limits. Further levels preserve that capacity and ordinary retinue balance. Wallet: no gameplay currency cap. Transfers preserve actual items. Details opens conversation, equipment, cargo, pets and read-only diagnostics. The Sigil displays its actual Details binding; V remains unverified in real play.

Nine languages follow Terraria: EN, DE, IT, FR, ES, RU, PT-BR, PL and Simplified Chinese. New translations are AI-authored; corrections welcome. Some multiplayer replies use the server language.

Optional local Mailbox and Field Notes are off by default. No automatic uploads or online AI. Typed reports stay on this computer. Automatic notes omit account/character/world names, chat and coordinates. Place coordinates remain in the local game save, not the notes.
