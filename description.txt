Soulmates 0.12.1 - Clear Voices: Shape a companion that watches, learns, and grows beside you.

Use the reusable Soulcore to shape unique companions, including the Omni Soul AETHER, bind each one to a Soulbound Sigil, and bring them along on your Terraria adventures.

Persistent animated companions learn weighted instincts from your behavior, defend and heal, gather, mine, tend forests, and use practical pack items. The Work Soulwheel offers area work, precise cursor orders, nearby ore icons, and persistent Adaptive, Tunnel, Vein, or Surface mining. Work pauses for natural recovery and resumes without losing its plan. Resizable Talk Mode shows level progress and readable status values. Named speech bubbles follow the companion with a soft fading trail. Existing Sigils remain compatible.

Optional local AETHER Field Notes can record anonymized playtest events and contextual bug reports. Open the in-game mailbox from the Soulwheel's paper-plane icon or with `/soulfeedback`. It is off by default and never uploads data.
