[h1]Create a companion with a soul.[/h1]

Soulmates turns the reusable Soulcore into a companion creator inside Terraria. Shape a distinct little spirit, bind it to its own Soulbound Sigil, summon it into the world, and build a relationship through care, conversation, and commands.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/reirao/Soulmates/main/media/soulmates-overview-0941.png[/img]

[url=https://github.com/reirao/Soulmates/releases/latest][b]View the complete screenshot gallery and latest release downloads on GitHub.[/b][/url]

[b]Languages:[/b] English and German. Soulmates follows Terraria's selected language automatically.

[h2]AI-Assisted Development Disclosure[/h2]
Soulmates is an experimental project created with extensive AI assistance across programming, writing, interface work, and visual development. The Workshop icon, mod icon, custom Soulkin sprite, promotional key art, and feature-board composition were AI-generated and then integrated into the mod. The screenshots inside the feature board are real in-game captures. The current Bunny, Blue Slime, Bird, Squirrel, item, and inventory visuals reuse Terraria assets as placeholders. Features are reviewed and tested in-game by the creator before release.

[h2]Create Your Soulmate[/h2]
[list]
[*]Choose a name, Bestiary muse, body form, essence, aura, personality, and one of five visible starting-talent classes.
[*]Watch the animated preview change as you shape the companion, including an original eight-frame Soulkin sprite that remains compatible with existing Sigils.
[*]Create multiple companions; every Soulbound Sigil keeps its own identity and stats.
[*]Switch freely between Sigils: exactly one companion remains active, and the previous companion's jobs, effects, and projectiles shut down cleanly.
[/list]

[h2]Getting Started[/h2]
New characters receive one Soulcore and three Blank Sigils as a prototype starter kit. Both items can also be crafted at a Work Bench.

[b]Soulcore:[/b] 8 Fallen Stars, 5 Amethyst, 10 Stone Blocks
[b]Blank Sigil:[/b] 3 Fallen Stars, 1 Amethyst, 5 Silk
[b]Starfinder Bell:[/b] 5 Fallen Stars, 3 Iron or Lead Bars, 1 Lens
[b]Delver Charm:[/b] 5 Iron or Lead Bars, 2 Amethyst, 20 Stone Blocks
[b]Hearth Ribbon:[/b] 5 Silk, 2 Daybloom, 2 Fallen Stars

[h2]Current Release[/h2]
Soulmates 0.9.4.1 gives single-player and multiplayer one coherent cargo path. The 12-acorn forestry reserve now covers every way items enter the pack, existing excess is safely returned to the owner, tree drops use normal visible gathering, and Soul creation is an atomic inventory operation with clear feedback. Existing Soulbound Sigils remain compatible.

[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*]Use Soulcore: open the Soul Creator.
[*]Use Soulbound Sigil: summon the bound companion.
[*]Hold Up + right-click active Sigil: recall the companion.
[*]Use a Soul Trinket while a companion is summoned: equip it.
[*]Right-click a Soul Trinket: remove the current trinket.
[*]Talk Mode > Pack: inspect cargo, store the selected hotbar item, or unload everything.
[*]Pack slots: left-click to take a stack, right-click to take one item.
[*]Right-click the companion or your own character: open the Soulwheel; left-click icon categories and their crescent actions.
[*]Choose the bag icon: open the companion pack directly.
[*]Choose Details on the Soulwheel, or press V by default: open the full Talk Mode.
[*]Press G by default: open the Soulwheel directly on its Terraria-emote categories.
[*]Choose Follow, Stay, or Explore: cancel the current area assignment.
[/list]
