[h1]Soulmates - AETHER-EGG Alpha[/h1]
Create a companion with a soul. The reusable Soulcore opens a creator inside Terraria; every Soulbound Sigil keeps one companion's name, appearance, personality, growth, memories, and cargo.

[b]Version: 0.15.0 - Familiar Faces[/b]
[b]Languages:[/b] English and German, following Terraria's language.
[url=https://github.com/reirao/Soulmates]Source and release notes[/url]

[h2]Start Together[/h2]
New characters receive one Soulcore and three Blank Sigils once. Choose a name, starting talent, personality, colors, aura, and an animated Soulkin, Bunny, Blue Slime, Bird, or Squirrel muse. Bind a Sigil and summon. Only one companion is active per player; switching Sigils retires the previous companion and its effects. Existing Sigils remain compatible.

[h2]Small Wheels, Direct Choices[/h2]
[list]
[*]Right-click the companion for commands, work, bonding, cargo, settings, full details, and the feedback mailbox.
[*]Right-click yourself for a Player wheel with Emotes, Point, and Mailbox. All 151 vanilla emotes unfold in crescent categories; G opens Emotes directly.
[*]Right-click clear air for world modes. Right-click cycles Point / Area; the native X exits. Look, Gather, Mine, and Forest remain active for repeated targets. Normal tile, NPC, inventory, and alternate-use interactions retain priority.
[*]Nearby mining expands into real ore-family icons. Mining approaches include Adaptive, Tunnel, Vein, and Surface, with tool-strength, energy, reach, and protected-tile checks.
[*]Answer initiative with Yes, No, Always, or Never. The answer wheel opens at the player while she shows the task and a question mark. Commands > Initiative Rules sets Ask / Always / Never separately for gathering, mining, forestry, and treasure.
[*]Talk Mode keeps conversations, memories, mood, energy, level progress, and cargo in a resizable detail view.
[/list]

[h2]Familiar Faces[/h2]
Companions greet nearby townsfolk, listen to their native emotes, and answer according to personality and the symbol's meaning. Residents become acquaintances, familiar company, friends, or someone to approach cautiously. These relationships persist on the Sigil and distinguish world, NPC type, and resident name.
[list]
[*]First meetings and friendship milestones become memories. Talk Mode > Bond > Who have you made friends with here? cycles her local acquaintances.
[*]Affection, laughter, sadness, anger, questions, work, and rest receive fitting native-symbol replies. Anger is no longer treated as a cheerful reward.
[*]Resident roles have their own dialogue. Visits respect disabled autonomy and give way to explicit work or defense.
[*]Richer named speech follows her with a restrained fading trail; emotes keep quiet moments expressive.
[/list]

[h2]A Helpful Little Soul[/h2]
She defends nearby threats, heals according to talent, retrieves usable drops, investigates treasure, and mines reachable ore and safe learned materials. Stay keeps a true world anchor. Energy recovers naturally; demanding work can pause and resume. Work reports completion or blockers.

Attention weighs distance, talent, learned habits, and waiting time, with independent task cooldowns. Learning adapts to repeated gathering, mining, forestry, combat, and exploration. This is local game logic, not an online AI service.

Forestry shakes trees and clears natural fallen logs. The Forester perk also replants real carried acorns. Living tree-branch pruning is not enabled. Useful cargo can supply weapons, light, food, recovery, and occasional crafting suggestions.

[h2]AETHER's Little Company[/h2]
A companion named AETHER has every starting-talent instinct and learned perk. Give her a bug net in her cargo: approved forestry can catch nearby natural butterflies, fireflies, and lightning bugs. Forestry > Always also permits idle catches. Player-released and statue-spawned insects are excluded.

The real catch enters her normal cargo transaction. Up to six carried insects orbit her using native animated sprites; withdrawing them removes the flock. This is cosmetic, not a combat swarm, and creates no extra critters or pet slots.

[h2]Grow and Carry[/h2]
Experience supports 20 levels and five bond ranks. Equipment keeps 8 slots, expanding through bond or Hearth Ribbon to 12, with up to 99 units per item type. Resources have 60 stack slots and 50 units per level and type: 500 at level 10, 1,000 at level 20. Collected coins use a separate uncapped Wallet and occupy no item slots.

Cargo uses native item icons, paging, tooltips, whole-stack or single-item withdrawals, and coin denomination conversion. Anything the player's inventory cannot accept stays with the companion. Existing Sigils preserve cargo and safely return level-reserve excess on summon.

[h2]Field Notes and Feedback[/h2]
The in-game AETHER Mailbox opens from the paper-plane icon or /soulfeedback. Optional Field Notes and contextual bug reports stay on this computer, are off by default, and never upload data.

This is a growing alpha. Automated checks and bounded client playtests are documented separately; full progression, long sessions, new visual combinations, and live multiplayer still need testing. All multiplayer participants need the same version. The creator has limited time to play, so reports about what happened and whether it was solo or multiplayer are especially welcome. We are equally happy to hear that a companion made you smile.

[h2]Development Disclosure[/h2]
Soulmates was created with extensive AI assistance in programming, writing, interfaces, and visual development. The Workshop icon, mod icon, and custom Soulkin sprite were AI-generated and integrated into the mod. Native Terraria sprites, items, inventory icons, emotes, and navigation remain in use. This disclosure is part of the project, not a claim that its gameplay uses an online AI.
