[h1]Create a companion with a soul.[/h1]

Soulmates turns the reusable Soulcore into a companion creator inside Terraria. Shape a distinct little spirit, bind it to its own Soulbound Sigil, summon it into the world, and build a relationship through care, conversation, and commands.

[b]Languages:[/b] English and German. Soulmates follows Terraria's selected language automatically.

[h2]AI-Assisted Development Disclosure[/h2]
Soulmates is an experimental project created with extensive AI assistance across programming, writing, interface work, and visual development. The Workshop icon, mod icon, and custom Soulkin sprite were AI-generated and then integrated into the mod. The current Bunny, Blue Slime, Bird, Squirrel, item, and inventory visuals reuse Terraria assets as placeholders. Features are reviewed and tested in-game by the creator before release.

[h2]A Companion With Character[/h2]
[list]
[*]Personality-driven idle, wandering, inspecting, following, and catch-up behavior.
[*]Persistent bond, mood, energy, and voice style.
[*]Persistent experience and 20 companion levels earned through combat, healing, work, and shared moments.
[*]A hold-and-release radial emote wheel with six personality-aware ways to wave, encourage, comfort, laugh, show affection, or rest together.
[*]Floating world speech and autonomous conversation shaped by personality, mood, weather, danger, and location.
[*]Distinct personality reactions to ordinary victories, bosses, and creature deaths.
[*]Five bond ranks that improve role strength, work radius, and late-rank pack capacity.
[*]Every companion continuously defends against nearby threats without spending work energy, even across cave terrain; Guardians react faster, reach farther, and hit harder, while Healers react to meaningful injuries.
[*]Energy regenerates naturally outside work and combat, and faster while waiting; asking for a rest is an optional boost.
[*]Talk Mode conversations across Care, Commands, Work, Bond, Voice, and Pack categories.
[*]Companions can accept a request, react in their own voice, or clearly refuse when tired or unhappy.
[*]Follow, stay, explore, rest, summon, and recall interactions.
[*]Area assignments: locate nearby chests, clear every early-ore vein in range, and retrieve every loose item in range.
[*]A persistent memory chronicle for creation, work, protection, healing, trinkets, and bond milestones.
[*]A persistent companion pack that holds normal and modded items across saves and summons.
[*]Interactive pack slots with item tooltips, stack withdrawal, selected-hotbar-item storage, and one-command unloading.
[*]A stronger soul light and local map discovery wherever the companion travels.
[*]Predictable refusals based on visible mood and energy instead of a random roll.
[*]Assignments stay saved during recalls, finish when the area is clear, and report when a new order is needed.
[*]A configurable Talk to Companion hotkey, set to V by default.
[*]Direct right-click interaction with the summoned companion.
[/list]

[h2]Getting Started[/h2]
New characters receive one Soulcore and three Blank Sigils as a prototype starter kit. Both items can also be crafted at a Work Bench.

[b]Soulcore:[/b] 8 Fallen Stars, 5 Amethyst, 10 Stone Blocks
[b]Blank Sigil:[/b] 3 Fallen Stars, 1 Amethyst, 5 Silk
[b]Starfinder Bell:[/b] 5 Fallen Stars, 3 Iron or Lead Bars, 1 Lens
[b]Delver Charm:[/b] 5 Iron or Lead Bars, 2 Amethyst, 20 Stone Blocks
[b]Hearth Ribbon:[/b] 5 Silk, 2 Daybloom, 2 Fallen Stars

[h2]Current Release[/h2]
Soulmates 0.7.0 Living Bond adds persistent companion levels, an expressive radial emote wheel, animated shared moments, autonomous floating conversation, and personality-aware reactions to combat and creature deaths. Stay now anchors companions to a real synchronized position while they defend it. The release retains server-authoritative multiplayer combat, natural energy recovery, autonomous Guardian and Healer roles, five bond ranks, memories, packs, area assignments, equipment, world interaction, and complete English/German localization.

[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*]Use Soulcore: open the Soul Creator.
[*]Use Soulbound Sigil: summon the bound companion.
[*]Right-click active Sigil: open Talk Mode.
[*]Hold Up + right-click active Sigil: recall the companion.
[*]Use a Soul Trinket while a companion is summoned: equip it.
[*]Right-click a Soul Trinket: remove the current trinket.
[*]Talk Mode > Pack: inspect cargo, store the selected hotbar item, or unload everything.
[*]Pack slots: left-click to take a stack, right-click to take one item.
[*]Press V by default: open Talk Mode directly while the companion is summoned.
[*]Hold G by default: point toward an emote and release to share it with the companion.
[*]Right-click the companion: open Talk Mode directly.
[*]Choose Follow, Stay, or Explore: cancel the current area assignment.
[/list]
