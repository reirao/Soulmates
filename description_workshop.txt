[h1]Soulmates - AETHER-EGG Alpha[/h1]
Create a companion with a soul. The reusable Soulcore opens a creator inside Terraria; each Soulbound Sigil keeps one companion's name, appearance, personality, growth, memories, and pack.

[b]Current version: 0.12.2 - Patient Choices[/b]
[b]Languages:[/b] English and German, following Terraria's language.
[url=https://github.com/reirao/Soulmates/releases/latest]Latest build, real gameplay screenshots, and release notes[/url]

[h2]Start Together[/h2]
New characters receive one Soulcore and three Blank Sigils once. Choose a name, personality, starting talent, colors, aura, and an animated Soulkin, Bunny, Blue Slime, Bird, or Squirrel muse. Bind a Sigil and use it to summon. Only one companion is active per player; switching Sigils retires the previous companion and its effects. Existing Sigils remain compatible.

[h2]Small Wheels, More Personality[/h2]
[list]
[*]Right-click the companion for commands, work, bonding, pack access, settings, and full details in its Soulwheel.
[*]Right-click your own character or press G for the separate Player Emote Wheel. All 151 vanilla emotes are grouped into crescent categories using Terraria graphics.
[*]Talk Mode retains conversations, memories, level progress, mood, energy, and cargo in a resizable detail view.
[*]Companions share native emotes, occasional personality-aware speech, and small encounters with town NPCs. Named speech follows the companion with a restrained fading trail.
[/list]

[h2]A Helpful Little Soul[/h2]
[list]
[*]Companions defend nearby, heal according to talent, retrieve usable loose drops, investigate treasure, and help with mining. Stay keeps a real world anchor rather than pulling them back to the player.
[*]Learning weighs repeated gathering, mining, forestry, combat, and exploration habits. It is game logic, not an online AI service.
[*]Initiative choices use Yes, No, Always, or Never, remembered separately for gathering, mining, forestry, and treasure. Autonomy can be toggled per companion.
[*]Give an area assignment or point at one material cluster or loose item. The mining wheel shows nearby ore families as real item icons.
[*]Choose Adaptive, Tunnel, Vein, or Surface mining. Pick-power, protected tiles, reach, and energy still limit what can be done. Unstable materials are worked only from exposed edges.
[*]Work pauses for natural energy recovery and resumes its plan. Completed or blocked assignments report their outcome.
[*]Forestry can shake trees and clear natural fallen logs; the learned Forester perk also supports careful acorn planting. Unproductive trees get a rest.
[*]Practical pack items can help: weapons strengthen attacks, torches add light, food and recovery items may be used, and carried materials can inspire an occasional crafting suggestion.
[*]Name a companion AETHER for an Omni Soul with every starting talent instinct and learned perk.
[/list]

[h2]Grow and Carry[/h2]
Companions earn experience through combat, healing, work, and shared moments across 20 levels. Five bond ranks improve their support. Energy recovers naturally; refusals explain energy or mood requirements. Their persistent pack holds 8 slots, or 12 with a Hearth Ribbon, with a reserve of 99 per item type and 12 acorns. Companions also illuminate their surroundings and reveal the nearby map.

[h2]What's New in 0.12.2[/h2]
The reusable Soulcore is no longer consumed by inventory right-click. Soulmates menus stop clicks from using the held item or world tile underneath. New initiative prompts wait for other menus and NPC conversations, keep their buttons stationary, and allow a minute to answer. Speech stays out of the inventory and vanilla NPC dialogue. This release also includes the 0.12.1 localization, speech-placement, UI scaling, cargo-hitbox, and mining-plan corrections.

[h2]Controls and Crafting[/h2]
[list]
[*]Use Soulcore: Creator. Use Sigil: summon. Hold Up + right-click the active Sigil: recall.
[*]Bag icon: pack. Details or V: Talk Mode. Paper-plane icon or /soulfeedback: local AETHER Mailbox.
[*]Pack slots: left-click takes a stack; right-click takes one item. Store the selected hotbar item or unload all from Pack.
[*]Follow, Stay, or Explore cancels the current area assignment. ASK AGAIN resets Always/Never choices.
[*]Use a trinket to equip it; right-click it to remove it. G and V are configurable keybindings.
[/list]
At a Work Bench: Soulcore = 8 Fallen Stars + 5 Amethyst + 10 Stone Blocks; Blank Sigil = 3 Fallen Stars + 1 Amethyst + 5 Silk; Hearth Ribbon = 5 Silk + 2 Daybloom + 2 Fallen Stars.
At an Anvil: Starfinder Bell = 5 Fallen Stars + 3 Iron/Lead Bars + 1 Lens; Delver Charm = 5 Iron/Lead Bars + 2 Amethyst + 20 Stone Blocks.

[h2]An Honest Growing Alpha[/h2]
Soulmates grew from a pet creator into a companion with work, cargo, learning, and native emotes. Real play exposed awkward controls and cargo problems, and several systems were rebuilt. The full history lives on GitHub, not repeated in every Workshop update.

Single-player and server-authoritative multiplayer are implemented. The latest checks passed 2,013 static/math and 2,669 isolated engine assertions. A real fresh-character playtest covered creation, pack transfers, emotes, tree shaking, combat, and healing. That does not certify every progression tier, long session, or multiplayer combination.

My playtime is limited and progression takes time. Please tell me what happened, your mod version, and whether you played solo or multiplayer when something feels strange. If a companion made you smile, I am just as happy to hear it. The optional AETHER Mailbox and Field Notes save feedback and bug context locally; recording is off by default and nothing is uploaded.
