[h1]Soulmates[/h1]
Create a companion with the reusable Soulcore, give her a name and a place in your world, and grow together. Her Soulbound Sigil keeps personality, appearance, progress, memories, cargo and pets.

[h1]YOUR COMPANION CAN BECOME THE BOSS[/h1]
[b]Yes, her. The same companion, with the same memories and inventory.[/b]
Turn on EVIL with the angry face directly on her wheel, then Games > secret eye. First click shows the warning; a second confirms a real owner-only duel. She grows, displays HP, telegraphs dashes and fires wider patterns across three health phases. Two seconds of preparation.

[b]Your character can take real damage.[/b] No loot/XP rewards, terrain destruction or permanent companion death. Her familiar/retinue rest; other players, residents and critters are not targets and other companions do not join. Stop via Games > Stop, Commands > Abort/Pause, EVIL off or recall. Victory, owner loss/death, leash, five-minute timeout or another boss also ends it. Shots/debuffs clear, inventory/memories survive; [b]work stays paused until Resume.[/b] Normal EVIL is optional theatrical banter, not destruction permission.

[h2]1. Create and Grow[/h2]
One-time Soulcore and three Blank Sigils; replacements are craftable. Choose name, personality, talent, colors, aura and Soulkin/Bunny/Slime/Bird/Squirrel form. Bind, summon and keep the Sigil in inventory. One companion per player; switching retires old effects. AETHER has all talents/perks.

[b]Open-ended exact XP/levels[/b] preserve old progress and the original curve. Five bond ranks, mood, recovering energy and learned insights add growth. Resources and ordinary retinue retain level-20 limits, not endless power.

[h2]2. Wheels and Commands[/h2]
[list]
[*]Right-click her/yourself for separate wheels. Details opens conversation, equipment and diagnostics; Pets/Pack open directly. Player Emotes categorizes all 151 vanilla symbols.
[*]Terraria mode protects native inventory, typing, NPC/chest, cursor-item and alternate actions. Me/Soulmate arms contextual pointing; X/Escape exits. Captured targets/replies reject stale identities. Workbench right-click opens crafting.
[*]Work: nearby ore, Mine area, Config and Last. Last repeats accepted intent; pointed jobs need a fresh target. Settings/failed orders do not replace it.
[*]Ask/Always/Never independently controls six abilities. Yes permits one opportunity; learning grants no permission. Pause retains, Resume continues, Abort cancels/holds automatic work. Defense remains outside the duel.
[/list]

[h2]3. A Useful Companion[/h2]
Follow, inspect, idle or Stay at a true anchor. Defense costs no work energy; Guardians react faster/farther, Healers spend energy. Real drops/mining output, treasure, light/map reveal and useful carried weapons, torches, food/recovery items support existing abilities. Occasional recipe ideas use materials.

Mining: Adaptive/Tunnel/Vein/Surface, ore/material filters, compass and bounded Short/next-opening routes. Tools, progression, plants, saplings, decorations, liquids and unstable terrain retain checks. Forester shakes eligible trees, clears fallen logs, gathers seeds/replants carried acorns. Pruning covers verified vanilla side twigs, not all trees. Targets/timeouts are rechecked; mood/energy recover with an exhaustion reserve.

[h2]4. Personality, Home and Memory[/h2]
Habits, requests and actual outcomes shape bounded Sigil preferences. Evaluations use the answer wheel; Later/silence/cancellation are neutral. Quiet disables personal questions. Safe idle visits to a classified home, her familiar or observed supplies become memories after completion/return. No autonomous building or copied architecture.

Home's house symbol offers our home/her home/just a stop/Later, including Stay/autonomy off/Quiet. Rooms, actual beds and resident housing are distinct evidence; familiarity is not ownership. Work/residents and changed rooms/worlds are respected; home chests are not new automatic treasure.

NPC/native-emote encounters build relationships. Do you remember? recalls real events or truthful current observations. Speech/emotes share attention. Games: independent Rock, paper, scissors, no XP; other minigames are not implemented.

[h2]5. Critters, Pets and Retinue[/h2]
[b]Watch[/b] greets; [b]Company[/b] invites supported real wild critters (one normally, AETHER three), preserving native physics/health/catching/despawning, not copies or teleports. [b]Collect[/b] has basic-net capability, not a free net item; lava species need a lava-proof net. Gold/statue/released creatures are protected. Consent, distance, visibility, energy/tasks apply. Catching and witnessed death differ; unknown causes accuse nobody.

[b]Pets:[/b] upper gift row is yours; twelve lower slots are hers. Give unfavorited reusable items, left-click an owned pet to summon, right-click to return. Only first gift into empty storage selects; later gifts respect dismissal. Icons select, not duplicate; keep the item with her. One harmless familiar is separate from your pet: native animation/basic light, custom movement, not original pet AI/modded drawing.

[b]Retinue:[/b] staff symbol opens real gifted-staff storage. Left-click calls, right-click returns. Normal companions select 1/2/3 distinct types at levels 1/10/20; [b]AETHER all owned distinct types simultaneously[/b], within 64 slots. Duplicates add no helpers. Native art, bounded custom combat, not original minion AI; your buffs/summon slots stay separate. Recall/switch/dismiss/final withdrawal clean up effects. Cosmetic insect flock requires real carried insects, not a free army.

[h2]6. Cargo and Character[/h2]
Equipment: 8-12 slots, 99 units/type. Resources: 60 slots, 50 units/level/type, max 1,000 from level 20; native stack limits apply. Wallet: exact uncapped balance, Here for you! offers. Real transfers preserve ownership/capacity; leftovers stay. MP conflicts reject, not overwrite. Tools are capabilities, not generic armor slots.

[h2]How It Works / Honest Beta[/h2]
Local activity selection, consent policies and persistent Sigils connect behavior. Bounded preferences are not online AI. Native NPC/projectile/item APIs handle real state; owner/session checks bind effects. Exact big-integer XP uses an integer square root. Boss mode temporarily transforms/restores the same NPC; pets/retinue stay item-backed, separate from player buffs.

Nine languages; AI-authored translations need corrections, some MP replies use server language. Engine/adversarial checks pass; full progression, endurance, connected MP and all visual families remain open. All peers need 0.27.0. V is unverified; use Details/Pets. Older media is historical, not boss footage.

[h2]Feedback and Transparency[/h2]

Typed notes keep your text; share only chosen reports. Please give version, action, expected/actual and solo/MP. Limited playtime makes your feedback valuable; happy moments welcome too!

AI assistance: code, writing, UI/visuals. Icons/Soulkin are AI-generated; other creature/item/inventory/emote art reuses Terraria. No online AI service.
