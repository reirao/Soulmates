[h1]Create a companion with a soul.[/h1]

Soulmates turns the reusable Soulcore into a companion creator inside Terraria. Shape a distinct little spirit, bind it to a Soulbound Sigil, and share care, conversation, work, and adventures.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/reirao/Soulmates/main/media/soulmates-overview.png[/img]

[url=https://github.com/reirao/Soulmates/releases/latest][b]Screenshots, changelog, and latest release on GitHub[/b][/url]

[b]Languages:[/b] English and German, selected automatically from Terraria's language.

[h2]AI-Assisted Development Disclosure[/h2]
Soulmates was created with extensive AI assistance across programming, writing, UI, and visual development. The Workshop art, mod icon, and Soulkin sprite were AI-generated and integrated into the mod. Bunny, Slime, Bird, Squirrel, item, inventory, and emote visuals use Terraria assets. The released build was reviewed and tested in-game by the creator.

[h2]Create Your Soulmate[/h2]
[list]
[*]Choose a name, Bestiary muse, body form, essence, aura, personality, and one of five visible starting classes.
[*]The animated preview shows your design, including the original eight-frame Soulkin sprite.
[*]Every Sigil stores its own identity and stats. When switching, exactly one companion stays active and old jobs, effects, and projectiles end cleanly.
[/list]

[h2]Soulwheel and Conversation[/h2]
[list]
[*]Right-click the companion for the compact Soulwheel with commands, work, bonding, pack access, and details.
[*]Right-click your character or press G for a separate Player Emote Wheel with all 151 vanilla emotes. Categories unfold as small crescent sub-wheels.
[*]Talk Mode keeps Care, Commands, Work, Bond, Voice, and Pack in one compact detail view.
[*]Companions speak according to personality, mood, weather, danger, and location, react to fights and creature deaths, and share small wordless moments with town NPCs.
[*]Speech remains readable longer, deliberately trails a departing soul, and fades as a soft echo where it was spoken.
[/list]

[h2]Autonomy With Character[/h2]
[list]
[*]Resting, wandering, exploring, following, catching up, and small surprises depend on personality. Autonomy can be toggled per companion.
[*]Initiative questions appear as an emote crescent. Choose Yes, No, Always, or Never separately for gathering, mining, forestry, and treasure hunting.
[*]Every companion retrieves nearby usable drops. Gatherers work farther and faster, Miners clear early ore, Treasure Seekers inspect chests, Guardians intercept danger, and Healers respond to injuries.
[*]Companions defend without spending work energy and stay near their owner or Stay anchor. Guardians react harder; Healers address meaningful injuries.
[*]Learned inclinations adapt to your gathering, mining, forestry, combat, and exploration habits.
[*]The Forester perk shakes trees, clears natural fallen logs, collects seeds, and carefully replants carried acorns.
[*]A companion named AETHER is an Omni Soul with every talent instinct and learned perk.
[*]Assignments survive recall, end when an area is clear, and report when a new order is needed.
[/list]

[h2]Grow and Remember[/h2]
[list]
[*]Persistent bond, mood, energy, voice, learning inclinations, memories, and 20 levels.
[*]Combat, healing, work, and shared moments grant experience. Five bond ranks improve role strength, work radius, and pack size.
[*]Energy regenerates naturally outside work and combat, and faster while waiting. Refusals visibly follow energy and mood instead of random rolls.
[*]The persistent pack carries normal and modded items across saves and summons: up to 99 per item type and 12 acorns.
[*]Pack slots include tooltips and stack withdrawal; store the selected hotbar item or unload everything at once.
[*]The companion provides soul light and reveals the local map wherever it travels.
[/list]

[h2]Getting Started[/h2]
New characters receive one Soulcore and three Blank Sigils. Everything can also be crafted.

[b]Soulcore:[/b] 8 Fallen Stars, 5 Amethyst, 10 Stone Blocks
[b]Blank Sigil:[/b] 3 Fallen Stars, 1 Amethyst, 5 Silk
[b]Starfinder Bell:[/b] 5 Fallen Stars, 3 Iron/Lead Bars, 1 Lens
[b]Delver Charm:[/b] 5 Iron/Lead Bars, 2 Amethyst, 20 Stone Blocks
[b]Hearth Ribbon:[/b] 5 Silk, 2 Daybloom, 2 Fallen Stars

[h2]How Soulmates Got Here[/h2]
Soulmates began as an item for shaping a pet. Repeated in-game tests turned it into persistent Sigils, personalities, combat support, work, cargo, learning, forestry, initiative, and native emotes. Some systems were rebuilt more than once when real play exposed awkward controls, disappearing cargo, duplicate drops, or stale abilities. The GitHub changelog preserves that history; this page describes only the implemented state.

[h2]Playtesting and Feedback[/h2]
Every report helps. My playtime is limited and Terraria progression takes time, so some combinations and long-session problems emerge slowly. If something is strange, unclear, or breaks later, please say what happened and whether it was single-player or multiplayer. If your companion simply made you smile, I am just as happy to hear that.

[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*]Use Soulcore: open Creator. Use Sigil: summon companion.
[*]Hold Up + right-click the active Sigil: recall.
[*]Right-click companion: Soulwheel. Right-click your character or press G: Player Emote Wheel.
[*]Bag icon: open pack directly. Details or V: open full Talk Mode.
[*]Pack slots: left-click takes a stack; right-click takes one item.
[*]Use Trinket: equip. Right-click Trinket: remove.
[*]Follow, Stay, or Explore cancels the area assignment.
[*]ASK AGAIN under Commands resets all Always/Never choices.
[/list]
