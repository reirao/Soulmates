[h1]Soulmates[/h1]
Create a personal companion with the reusable Soulcore. Her Soulbound Sigil keeps appearance, personality, growth, memories and cargo.

[b]Version: 0.22.1 - Showcase Repairs | Beta Candidate[/b]
This patch repairs cargo clicks, unanswered-question fading, Soulcore animation and diagnostic labels; speech avoids the corner minimap when space allows.
[b]Languages:[/b] EN, DE, IT, FR, ES, RU, PT-BR, PL and Simplified Chinese. Follows Terraria. New translations are AI-authored; corrections welcome. Some MP replies still use the server language.
[url=https://github.com/reirao/Soulmates]Source and release notes[/url]

[h2]Start Together[/h2]
Characters receive a Soulcore and three Blank Sigils once. Use the Soulcore, choose name, talent, personality, colors, aura and Soulkin, Bunny, Slime, Bird or Squirrel appearance, then bind a Sigil. Use it to summon her and keep it in your inventory. One companion per player; switching retires old effects. Soulcore stays reusable. Existing Sigils remain compatible.

[h2]Small Wheels, Clear Intentions[/h2]
[list]
[*]Right-click her for tools; yourself for Emotes, Point and Mailbox. Details/V opens Conversation, Equipment and Diagnostics with portrait, vitals, replies, cargo and pet choices. Tools display capabilities, not armor slots. G opens all 151 native emotes in categories. Bond groups Feelings, Gestures and Care; Games has Rock, paper, scissors.
[*]Work opens Config and Last. Config unfolds area/pointed work, mining modes and filters. Last remembers accepted work per Sigil; failed orders, Look and setting changes do not replace it. Pointed work asks for a fresh target, not an old position or item slot.
[*]Mining Mode offers Adaptive, Tunnel, Vein and Surface. Tunnel compass: Up/Right/Down/Left/Auto. Short: eight steps; Until opening: up to 24, bounded by work radius. Cardinal routes stop at liquid, unstable material, protected blocks and decorations, also after planning. Config starts no work; changes replan active area mining.
[*]Automatic ore/material filters use native icons and paging. Ores start allowed; observed terrain needs opt-in. Direct orders never bypass plant protection, tool strength or progression rules.
[*]Terraria is the default mouse mode: native interactions first. Unhandled nearby NPC/drop/resource clicks open context; air and furniture do not. Me/Soulmate arms world right-click. Targets stay fixed while choosing. Right-click in a wheel cycles modes; X/Escape exits. Inventory, typing, NPC dialogue and cursor-held items keep priority.
[*]Commands > Initiative Rules sets Ask / Always / Never separately for gathering, mining, forestry, treasure, Company and Collect. Yes approves one target only. Direct orders and Last do not grant automatic permission. Pause retains work, Resume continues it, Abort clears it and holds automatic work. Defense still works.
[/list]

[h2]A Helpful Little Soul[/h2]
She defends, heals by talent, retrieves real drops, mines safe materials and investigates treasure. Stay anchors her. Energy recovers during following and work; effort is charged in batches. Exhaustion rebuilds a reserve before resuming; Care > Rest is optional.

Company/Collect share attention with work; personal questions do not block approved Always visits. Ask/Never still apply. Learning is local game logic, not online AI. Defense, pause and assignments keep priority.

Forester shakes supported trees, clears fallen logs and replants real carried acorns. A carried axe allows verified dry side-twig pruning; living/unsupported parts stay protected. Equipment can support attacks, light, food, recovery and crafting suggestions.

[h2]Critters Are Not Item Pets[/h2]
Watch observes and greets without catching. Company invites one real natural critter, or three for AETHER. Native collisions, health and despawning remain; no copies, noclip or teleporting. Invited animals stay with her when autonomy is disabled. Ground followers can hop at small obstacles; Pause stops guided movement. Off/recall releases them.

Catch Critters (formerly Pet Collect) includes a basic net capability, not a withdrawable item. Lava critters need a carried lava-proof net. Native catching and cargo limits apply; gold, statue-spawned and released creatures are protected.

Clicked eligible creatures offer Look, Company and Collect. Explicit visits work even with autonomy off, without granting future consent. Automatic visits still need permission, energy and free attention. Hover/selection feedback shows watching, follower count, pause, busy/manual state or consent. Native critter greetings pair creature/gesture symbols with a reply. Witnessed deaths can prompt queued grief or disapproval; catches are not deaths. Live timing still needs testing.

Critters > Companion pet selects a real Zephyr Fish/Nectar item in her Equipment pack. One harmless Zephyr Fish or Baby Hornet follows her alongside your own pet. The item stays in cargo, unconsumed; Dismiss, withdrawal and recall remove the follower. Selection persists per Sigil. Native animated art uses companion-following movement, not vanilla player-pet AI. Only these two flying pets are supported; light, ground and modded pets are not.

AETHER has every talent/perk; up to six real carried insects form a cosmetic flock. Appearance, real Company animals, item-backed pets and the flock are separate. No combat swarm or player pet/buff replacement.

[h2]Remember, Answer, Carry[/h2]
Town visits use native emotes and personality-aware replies. Resident relationships and real shared memories persist. Talk > Items discusses categories and held examples; "Do you remember?" recalls actual moments. Speech stays near her; unanswered questions hold its fade. Personal symbol answers affect mood, bond and saved tone without approving unrelated work. Quiet/Calm/Chatty adjusts personal questions, not permission prompts.

20 levels and five bond ranks. Equipment has 8-12 slots and a 99-unit reserve per type. Resources have 60 stack slots and 50 units per level and type: 500 at level 10, 1,000 at level 20. Wallet money is uncapped. Native stack limits apply. Withdraw stacks or individual items; anything that cannot fit stays. "Here for you!" offers savings to accept, keep or defer. Multiplayer inventory conflicts reject transfers instead of overwriting slots.

[h2]Feedback and Current Scope[/h2]
Diagnostics (wrench tab) shows live activity, permissions, stamina, nearby critter gates and owned pets. Export writes SoulmatesFeedback/diagnostic-latest.txt locally, without enabling recording or uploading anything. In MP the view labels client state, not server history.
AETHER Mailbox: paper-plane icon or /soulfeedback. Optional Field Notes are off by default. Everything stays local; nothing uploads or automatically reaches the developer. Automatic records omit names, chat and exact positions; typed notes keep your text. Share only notes you want reviewed.

Beta candidate: the creator reports a good playtest; critter timing, progression, endurance and connected MP still need tests. All peers need 0.22.1. Report action, expected/actual result and solo/MP; happy moments too.
