[h1]Soulmates[/h1]
Create a personal companion with the reusable Soulcore. Her Soulbound Sigil keeps identity, appearance, personality, growth, memories and cargo.

[b]Version: 0.20.0 - Shared Instincts | Beta Candidate[/b]
[b]Languages:[/b] EN, DE, IT, FR, ES, RU, PT-BR, PL, Simplified Chinese. Follows Terraria's language. New translations are AI-authored; corrections welcome. Some multiplayer replies still use the server language.
[url=https://github.com/reirao/Soulmates]Source and release notes[/url]

[h2]Start Together[/h2]
New characters receive one Soulcore and three Blank Sigils once. Use the Soulcore, choose a name, talent, personality, colors, aura and Soulkin, Bunny, Slime, Bird or Squirrel muse, then bind a Sigil. Use that Sigil to summon her. The Soulcore remains reusable. One companion per player; switching retires old effects. Keep the active Sigil in your inventory. Existing Sigils remain compatible.

[h2]Small Wheels, Direct Choices[/h2]
[list]
[*]Right-click her: commands, work, critters, bond, Games, cargo, Items, Details and Mailbox. Details or V opens full Talk Mode with stats and stories.
[*]Games > Rock, paper, scissors uses three native symbols, independent choices and personality-aware replies. No XP farming; Bond keeps its gestures.
[*]Right-click yourself: Emotes, Point and Mailbox. G opens all 151 native emotes. Emotes > Items groups Food, Recovery, Tools, Weapons, Materials, Valuables and Party. Her Items branch has separate cargo topics, including Ores.
[*]Terraria is the default: native interactions come first. Unhandled nearby NPC/drop/resource clicks open context; air and furniture do not. Me/Soulmate arms the next world right-click. The clicked target stays fixed while choosing. Right-click in a wheel cycles modes; X/Escape exits. Inventory, typing, NPC dialogue and cursor-held items keep priority.
[*]Work > Mining Mode offers Adaptive, Tunnel, Vein and Surface. Automatic mining has paged ore/material switches: ores start allowed, observed terrain needs opt-in. Rules persist. Direct orders bypass only automatic filters, never plant/decor protection or native tool requirements.
[*]Commands > Initiative Rules sets Ask / Always / Never independently for gathering, mining, forestry, treasure, critter Company and Collect. Reply Yes, No, Always or Never to the task/question symbols. Yes approves one target; direct orders do not approve future automatic visits. Old Sigils start new critter rules at Ask.
[*]Pause keeps work, Resume continues, Abort clears it and holds automatic work until resumed or replaced. Defense still works. Commands > Personal questions selects Quiet, Calm or Chatty without disabling work-permission prompts.
[/list]

[h2]Familiar Faces[/h2]
Companions greet townsfolk and reply to native emotes with personality. World/resident relationships and actual shared events persist. Talk Mode > Bond recalls friends; "Do you remember?" tells their chapters. Items discusses real cargo or held examples. Long replies scroll; remarks wait behind speech. Text stays near her with a fading trail. Inspection does not consume cargo, change work permissions or grant XP.

[h2]A Helpful Little Soul[/h2]
She defends, heals by talent, gathers, investigates treasure and mines safe materials. Stay anchors her in the world. Energy recovers during following and work; effort is charged in batches. Exhaustion rebuilds a 30-point reserve before resuming. Combat pauses recovery; Care > Rest is optional.

Attention weighs distance, talent, learned habits and waiting time. Learning uses local game logic, not online AI.

Defense, pause and retained work have priority. Approved pickup respects reservations, native delays, capacity, consent and Stay. Interrupted work keeps its state.

Forester shakes supported trees, clears fallen logs and replants real carried acorns. An axe allows dry vanilla side-twig pruning; living/unsupported parts stay protected. Cargo supports weapons, light, food, recovery and crafting suggestions.

[h2]Critters and Company[/h2]
Critters offers Watch, Company, Pet Collect and Off. Watch observes and greets without catching. Creature > greeting symbols pair with a native critter reply, queued behind speech/questions. Pet Collect has a built-in basic net capability: no purchased net, cargo slot or free withdrawable net item. Lava critters need a carried lava-proof upgrade. Cargo limits apply. Gold, statue-spawned and player-released creatures are protected.

Company guides one real natural critter, or three for AETHER. Native physics, health, catching and despawning remain. Off/recall releases them; no copies or teleporting.

Clicked eligible critters offer Look, Company and Collect. Direct visits replace current work; automatic visits need consent, free attention and energy. Witnessed deaths can prompt grief or disapproval, queued behind speech; catches are not deaths. Live timing still needs testing. No XP/penalties; Soul Bolts spare harmless critters.

AETHER has every starting-talent instinct and learned perk. Up to six carried insects form a cosmetic native-sprite flock; withdrawal removes it. No combat swarm; vanilla vanity pets remain player-bound.

[h2]Answers That Matter[/h2]
Personal questions offer gathering, playful/gentle company or Later. Symbols affect saved tone, mood and bond. Gathering never approves mining/forestry; Later has no penalty.

[h2]Grow and Carry[/h2]
20 levels, five bond ranks. Equipment has 8-12 slots and a 99-unit reserve per type. Resources have 60 stack slots and 50 units per level and type: 500 at level 10, 1,000 at level 20. Coins occupy a separate uncapped Wallet.

Item icons, pages and tooltips show cargo. Withdraw a stack or one item; anything that cannot fit stays. Old Sigils return excess reserves on summon. "Here for you!" offers savings: accept, save, reply playfully or defer.

[h2]Field Notes and Feedback[/h2]
The AETHER Mailbox opens from the paper-plane icon or /soulfeedback. Optional Field Notes are off by default. Notes and contextual bug reports stay local; nothing uploads or automatically reaches the developer. Automatic records omit names, chat and exact positions; typed notes keep your text. Share only the note you want reviewed.

Published beta candidate: first creator playtest broadly works, not full acceptance. Controls, critter reactions, progression, long sessions and connected MP need tests. All peers need 0.20.0. Report action, expected/actual result and solo/MP. Limited playtime makes reports valuable; happy moments too. Gallery footage: October 1, not new 0.20.0 captures.
