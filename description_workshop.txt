[h1]Soulmates[/h1]
Create a personal companion with the reusable Soulcore. Her Soulbound Sigil keeps appearance, personality, growth, memories, cargo and pets.

[h2]Start Together[/h2]
Characters receive a Soulcore and three Blank Sigils once. Choose name, talent, personality, colors, aura and Soulkin, Bunny, Slime, Bird or Squirrel appearance, then bind a Sigil. Use it to summon her and keep it in your inventory. One companion per player; switching retires old effects. Soulcore stays reusable. AETHER has all talents and learned perks.

[h2]A Place to Belong[/h2]
Enclosed furnished rooms, your actual valid selected bed and assigned resident housing provide different evidence. Bounded place memory is per Sigil and world. Separated returns can make a shelter familiar; standing still does not add visits. Familiar does not mean owned.

[b]Home[/b] on her wheel uses Terraria's house symbol. Discuss a valid shelter immediately or reclassify a remembered home: our home, her little home, just a stop or Later. Works at Stay, with autonomy off or Quiet questions. Ongoing work, resident housing and unanswered questions are respected. Replies recheck the captured world and room. Recognized home chests are not automatically rediscovered as treasure; explicit treasure search still works. She does not construct or remodel houses.

[h2]Optional EVIL[/h2]
Off by default, saved per Sigil. Toggle in Commands or the fourth task button in Details (laughing face). Safe idle banter, celebrations of actual work and theatrical Rock, paper, scissors turn little successes into tiny grand schemes. Presentation only: consent, tasks, rewards, cargo, damage, terrain and critter empathy stay intact. Higher-priority attention comes first.

[h2]Small Wheels, Clear Intentions[/h2]
[list]
[*]Right-click her for companion tools; yourself for player tools and categorized native Emotes. Details opens conversation, equipment, cargo, pets and read-only diagnostics. Pets opens directly from her wheel too. The Sigil shows the actual Details binding; V remains unverified in real play.
[*]Terraria is the default mouse mode: native interactions first. Inventory, typing, NPC dialogue, cursor-held items and alternate item actions retain priority. Me/Soulmate arms explicit world pointing; X/Escape exits. Stale companion/Sigil targets and replies are rejected. Answer symbols fade at fixed positions.
[*]Work offers nearby ore search, area mining, Config and Last. Last remembers accepted work per Sigil; pointed work needs a fresh target. Mining offers Adaptive, Tunnel, Vein and Surface, a bounded compass and ore/material filters. Plants, saplings, decorations, liquids, tools and progression remain protected. Rapid MP compass edits preserve consecutive choices.
[*]Initiative Rules sets Ask/Always/Never separately for gathering, mining, forestry, treasure, Company and Collect. Yes permits one opportunity. Direct orders and Last grant no automatic permission. Pause retains work, Resume continues and Abort cancels it and holds automatic work. Defense remains available.
[/list]

[h2]Useful Company[/h2]
She defends, heals by talent, retrieves real drops, mines safely and tends forests. Low energy and mood recover naturally; exhaustion needs a reserve. Jobs recheck live targets and bounded timeouts. Real threats take priority over training dummies.

Existing abilities learn modest contextual preferences from observations, accepted requests and actual outcomes. Occasional evaluations use the answer wheel; Later, silence and cancellation are neutral. Quiet disables automatic personal questions. Preferences never grant permission. Construction-item use gives context, not proof of placement or autonomous building. Residents, shared memories and item conversations use native emotes and personal replies. Local game logic, no online AI.

[h2]Critters and Item Pets[/h2]
Watch greets without catching. Company invites supported real wild critters with bounded native movement; AETHER up to three. Collect includes ordinary net capability; lava critters need an eligible lava-proof net. Gold, statue-spawned and released creatures remain protected. Distance, visibility, permissions, tasks and recovery still matter. Catching and death have separate reactions. Off/recall releases invited creatures.

Give real reusable vanity/light summon items through Pets. Favorites stay with you. Twelve owned slots: left-click summons, right-click returns the item. The first gift selects only if storage is empty; later gifts preserve selection/dismissal. One harmless familiar follows her separately from your pet. Native artwork uses custom movement/basic light, not original player-pet AI, special abilities or arbitrary modded drawing. AETHER's carried insects can form a separate cosmetic swarm.

[h2]Carry and Remember[/h2]
20 levels, five bond ranks. Equipment: 8-12 slots, 99 units per type. Resources: 60 slots, 50 units per level/type, up to 1,000 at level 20. Wallet: no gameplay currency cap. Native stack limits apply; transfers preserve real items and leave anything that does not fit. Savings can be offered to you. MP inventory conflicts reject transfers instead of overwriting items.
