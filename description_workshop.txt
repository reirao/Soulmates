[h1]Soulmates[/h1]
Create a companion with a soul. The reusable Soulcore opens a creator inside Terraria; every Soulbound Sigil keeps one companion's name, appearance, personality, growth, memories, and cargo.

[b]Version: 0.19.1 - Clear Choices | Growing Alpha[/b]
[b]Languages:[/b] EN, DE, IT, FR, ES, RU, PT-BR, PL, Simplified Chinese. Follows Terraria's language. New translations are AI-authored; corrections welcome. Some multiplayer replies still use the server language.
[url=https://github.com/reirao/Soulmates]Source and release notes[/url]

[h2]Start Together[/h2]
New characters receive one Soulcore and three Blank Sigils once. Choose name, talent, personality, colors, aura and an animated Soulkin, Bunny, Blue Slime, Bird or Squirrel muse. One companion per player; switching Sigils retires old effects. Keep the active Sigil in your inventory or she recalls. Existing Sigils remain compatible.

[h2]Small Wheels, Direct Choices[/h2]
[list]
[*]Right-click the companion for commands, work, bonding, cargo, settings, full details, and the feedback mailbox.
[*]Bond > Rock, paper, scissors unfolds three native symbols for real rounds and personality-aware reactions. No XP farming or interrupted assignments.
[*]Right-click yourself for a Player wheel with Emotes, Point, and Mailbox. All 151 vanilla emotes unfold in crescent categories; G opens Emotes directly.
[*]Terraria stays the default. Unhandled clicks on nearby NPCs, drops and resources open their context; air and furniture do not. Selecting Me or Soulmate closes the wheel and arms the next world right-click. Actions retain the clicked target; eligible critters offer Company without a net.
[*]Right-click inside an open wheel cycles modes; X or Escape restores normal use. Inventory, typing, NPC dialogue, and cursor-held items retain priority. Changed targets are rejected rather than silently redirecting orders.
[*]Nearby mining expands into real ore-family icons. Mining approaches include Adaptive, Tunnel, Vein, and Surface, with tool-strength, energy, reach, and protected-tile checks.
[*]Answer initiative with Yes, No, Always, or Never. The answer wheel opens at the player while she shows the task and a question mark. Commands > Initiative Rules sets Ask / Always / Never separately for gathering, mining, forestry, and treasure.
[*]Commands > Pause retains work; Resume continues it; Abort clears it and holds automatic work until resumed or replaced. Defense stays available.
[*]Companion wheel > Items opens native-icon Food, Ores, Tools and other topics. Topic clicks answer immediately. Talk Mode includes scrollable stories, mood, energy, levels and cargo.
[/list]

[h2]Familiar Faces[/h2]
Companions greet townsfolk and answer native emotes according to personality and meaning, including comfort and disagreement. Residents become acquaintances, friends, or someone to approach cautiously. Relationships and milestones persist on the Sigil, scoped by world, NPC type, and name. Talk Mode > Bond recalls her local acquaintances.

Resident roles have their own dialogue. Visits respect disabled autonomy and give way to work or defense. Named speech follows her with a fading trail; native emotes keep quiet moments expressive.

"Do you remember?" recalls real shared events with personality-aware reflections. Items discusses actual cargo or held examples by food, ore, tool, weapon, recovery, material and critter categories. Occasional remarks reflect visible surroundings and wait behind speech. Inspection and stories change no assignment, cargo, terrain, XP or permissions.

[h2]A Helpful Little Soul[/h2]
She defends nearby threats, heals according to talent, retrieves usable drops, investigates treasure, and mines reachable ore and safe learned materials. Stay keeps a true world anchor. Energy recovers naturally; demanding work can pause and resume. Work reports completion or blockers.

Attention weighs distance, talent, habits and waiting time. Learning adapts to gathering, mining, forestry, combat and exploration. Local game logic, not online AI.

Automatic critter visits yield to work. During Follow, approved nearby drops use the shared cargo transaction. Ask / Always / Never, native item reservations, pickup delays, cargo limits and Stay remain respected.

Forestry shakes trees, clears fallen logs and replants carried acorns with Forester. A real axe allows pruning dry vanilla side branches; Forester/AETHER can do it autonomously. Leafy branches, stems, ground and unsupported tree families stay protected. Cargo can supply weapons, light, food, recovery and crafting suggestions.

[h2]Critters and Company[/h2]
Critters offers Watch, Company, Pet Collect and Off. Passive Watch greets natural creatures even during work. Pet Collect needs a carried net and cargo room; lava-net rules apply. Gold, statue-spawned and released critters are excluded from automatic catches and company.

Company guides natural bunnies, squirrels, birds, butterflies, fireflies, and lightning bugs: one normally, up to three for AETHER. Real world NPCs retain collision, health, catching, and despawning. Off or recall releases them; no spawning or teleporting through terrain.

Clicked critters offer Look, Company and Collect. Direct visits replace current work; automatic visits wait for free attention, energy and mood. Autonomy, defense and pending questions remain respected. Visible deaths receive grief or disapproval; catches are not deaths. Reactions wait behind speech and grant no XP or penalties. Soul Bolts spare harmless critters.

AETHER has every starting-talent instinct and learned perk. Up to six carried insects form a cosmetic native-sprite flock; withdrawal removes it. No combat swarm; vanilla vanity pets remain player-bound.

[h2]Answers That Matter[/h2]
Four symbol answers to personal questions offer ongoing gathering, playful or gentle company, or talking later. They affect saved voice, mood, and bond. Only Gathering approves further collection, not mining or forestry. Later has no penalty.

[h2]Grow and Carry[/h2]
20 levels, five bond ranks. Equipment has 8-12 slots and a 99-unit reserve per type. Resources have 60 stack slots and 50 units per level and type: 500 at level 10, 1,000 at level 20. Coins occupy a separate uncapped Wallet.

Native item icons, paging, tooltips and stack/single-item withdrawals keep cargo accessible. Anything that cannot fit stays safe; old Sigils return excess level reserves on summon. "Here for you!" offers new savings: accept, save, reply playfully or defer. Money has no cap.

[h2]Field Notes and Feedback[/h2]
The in-game AETHER Mailbox opens from the paper-plane icon or /soulfeedback. Optional Field Notes and contextual bug reports stay on this computer, are off by default, and never upload data. Automatic records omit names, chat, and exact positions; your typed notes retain what you write.

Growing alpha: progression, long sessions and live multiplayer need tests. All players need the same version. Current reports: Emotes > Items is still flat; Company visits can be overshadowed by work. The creator has little playtime: report the action, result and solo/MP. We also love hearing that a companion made you smile.
