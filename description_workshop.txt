[h1]Create a companion with a soul.[/h1]

Soulmates turns the reusable Soulcore into a companion creator inside Terraria. Shape a distinct little spirit, bind it to its own Soulbound Sigil, summon it into the world, and build a relationship through care, conversation, and commands.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/reirao/Soulmates/main/media/soulmates-overview.png[/img]

[url=https://github.com/reirao/Soulmates/releases/latest][b]View the complete screenshot gallery and latest release downloads on GitHub.[/b][/url]

[b]Languages:[/b] English and German. Soulmates follows Terraria's selected language automatically.

[h2]AI-Assisted Development Disclosure[/h2]
Soulmates is an experimental project created with extensive AI assistance across programming, writing, interface work, and visual development. The Workshop icon, mod icon, and custom Soulkin sprite were AI-generated and then integrated into the mod. Bunny, Blue Slime, Bird, Squirrel, item, inventory, and emote visuals reuse Terraria assets. The released build was reviewed and tested in-game by the creator.

[h2]Create Your Soulmate[/h2]

[*]Choose a name, Bestiary muse, body form, essence, aura, personality, and one of five visible starting-talent classes.

[h2]Getting Started[/h2]
New characters receive one Soulcore and three Blank Sigils as a starter kit. Both items can also be crafted at a Work Bench.

[b]Soulcore:[/b] 8 Fallen Stars, 5 Amethyst, 10 Stone Blocks
[b]Blank Sigil:[/b] 3 Fallen Stars, 1 Amethyst, 5 Silk
[b]Starfinder Bell:[/b] 5 Fallen Stars, 3 Iron or Lead Bars, 1 Lens
[b]Delver Charm:[/b] 5 Iron or Lead Bars, 2 Amethyst, 20 Stone Blocks
[b]Hearth Ribbon:[/b] 5 Silk, 2 Daybloom, 2 Fallen Stars

[h2]Current Release[/h2]
Soulmates 9.9.0.23 rebuilds Companion, Player Emote, and initiative wheels around Terraria's own inventory slots, items, and animated emotes. Talk Mode is now a smaller detail view with symbolic tabs. Companion speech stays readable longer, deliberately trails a moving soul, and fades as a soft echo where it was spoken. This release also includes the coherent cargo path, persistent Yes / No / Always / Never initiative rules, tree-shake terrain fix, restored player right-click wheel, and corrected Squirrel facing. Existing Soulbound Sigils remain compatible.

[h2]How Soulmates Got Here[/h2]
Soulmates began as a reusable item for shaping a personal pet. Repeated in-game passes turned it into persistent Sigils, personalities, combat support, work assignments, a real pack, learned behavior, forestry, initiative choices, native emotes, and the compact Soulwheel. Some systems were rebuilt more than once when real play exposed awkward controls, disappearing cargo, duplicate drops, stale abilities, or behavior that did not remain coherent over a longer session. The GitHub changelog preserves that history; this description reflects only the implemented state of 9.9.0.23.

[h2]Playtesting and Feedback[/h2]
Every report helps. My playtime is limited and Terraria progression takes time, so some combinations and long-session behavior are discovered slowly. If something behaves strangely, feels unclear, or breaks later in a playthrough, please say what happened and whether it was single-player or multiplayer. And if a companion simply made you smile, I am just as happy to hear that.

[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*]Use Soulcore: open the Soul Creator.
[*]Use Soulbound Sigil: summon the bound companion.
[*]Hold Up + right-click active Sigil: recall the companion.
[*]Use a Soul Trinket while a companion is summoned: equip it.
[*]Right-click a Soul Trinket: remove the current trinket.
[*]Talk Mode > Pack: inspect cargo, store the selected hotbar item, or unload everything.
[*]Pack slots: left-click to take a stack, right-click to take one item.
[*]Right-click the companion or your own character: open the Soulwheel; left-click icon categories and their crescent actions.
[*]Choose the bag icon: open the companion pack directly.
[*]Choose Details on the Soulwheel, or press V by default: open the full Talk Mode.
[*]Press G by default: open the Player Emote Wheel.
[*]Choose Follow, Stay, or Explore: cancel the current area assignment.
[*]Choose ASK AGAIN under Commands: reset all remembered Always and Never initiative choices.
[/list]
