[h1]Soulmates[/h1]
Create a companion with the reusable Soulcore, give her a name and a place in your world, and grow together. Her Soulbound Sigil keeps personality, appearance, progress, memories, cargo and pets.

[b]0.28.0 - Careful Finds | Beta[/b]
New: consent-first salvage and individual critter encounters. Uploaded October 12. Upload, Steam approval and subscriber delivery are separate; check the loaded version under Mods.

[h1]YOUR COMPANION CAN BECOME THE BOSS[/h1]
EVIL's angry face > Games > secret eye, then confirm: the same companion becomes a three-phase boss with HP, telegraphed dashes, fan attacks and two-second warmup.

[b]Your character can take real damage.[/b] No loot/XP, terrain destruction or permanent companion death. Familiar/retinue rest; other players, residents and critters are not targets. End via Games > Stop, Commands > Abort/Pause, EVIL off or recall; also victory, owner loss/death, distance, five-minute timeout or another boss. Shots/debuffs clear; items/memories survive. [b]Work stays paused until Resume.[/b] Normal EVIL is theatrical banter, not destruction permission.

[h2]1. Create and Grow[/h2]
Soulcore and three Blank Sigils arrive once; replacements are craftable. Choose name, personality, talent, colors, aura and Soulkin/Bunny/Slime/Bird/Squirrel. Keep the active Sigil in inventory. One companion per player; switching retires old effects. AETHER has all talents/perks.

Open-ended exact XP/levels preserve progress. Bond, mood, energy and insights add growth; resources/ordinary retinue retain level-20 caps.

[h2]2. Wheels and Commands[/h2]
[list]
[*]Right-click her/yourself for separate wheels. Details opens conversation, equipment and diagnostics; Pets/Pack open directly. Player Emotes categorizes all 151 vanilla symbols.
[*]Terraria mode protects native interactions. Me/Soulmate arms contextual pointing; X/Escape exits. Replies reject stale targets. Workbench right-click opens crafting.
[*]Work: nearby ore, Mine area, Config and Last. Last repeats accepted intent; pointed jobs need a fresh target. Settings/failed orders do not replace it.
[*]Ask/Always/Never separately controls six abilities; learning grants no permission. Pause retains, Resume continues, Abort cancels/holds automatic work. Defense remains outside the duel. Salvage and individual catching require specific consent.
[/list]

[h2]3. Useful Work and Careful Salvage[/h2]
Follow, inspect, idle or Stay. Defense costs no work energy; Guardians react faster/farther, Healers spend energy. Real pickup, treasure, light/map reveal, useful carried items and occasional recipe ideas support existing abilities.

Mining: Adaptive/Tunnel/Vein/Surface, ore/material filters, compass and bounded short/next-opening routes. Tools, progression, plants, saplings, decorations, liquids and unstable terrain retain checks. Forester shakes eligible trees, clears fallen logs, gathers seeds/replants carried acorns. Pruning covers verified vanilla side twigs, not all trees. Mood/energy recover with an exhaustion reserve.

[b]Salvage:[/b] a find's context wheel asks for only this object or matching nearby finds. Nearby needs another unknown-origin confirmation: at most 16 captured same-type/style objects within 12 tiles, never future finds. Gems, pots, cobwebs, coral, books, bottles, water candles, statues, banners and five painting sizes are supported, [b]not arbitrary furniture[/b]. Native tool progression applies.

Observed construction, recognized homes, beds, housing, wiring, paint/coatings, tile entities, unsafe supports and neighboring decorations block removal. Whole objects/surroundings are rechecked before native destruction; real drops use ordinary pickup. Construction evidence persists in world saves. [b]Old unrecorded objects are unknown, not proven natural.[/b] Direct tile-writing mods may bypass observation. Pause/Resume/Abort apply; no automatic demolition permission.

[h2]4. Personality, Home and Memory[/h2]
Habits and real outcomes shape bounded Sigil preferences. Answer-wheel evaluations guide them; Later/silence/cancellation are neutral. Quiet disables personal questions. Completed safe home/familiar/supply visits become memories. No autonomous building.

Home's house offers our home/her home/just a stop/Later, also at Stay/autonomy off/Quiet. Rooms, beds and housing provide distinct evidence; familiarity is not ownership. Changes/residents are respected; home chests are not automatic treasure.

NPC/emote encounters build bounded relationships. Do you remember? recalls real events/observations. Speech/emotes share attention. Games includes independent Rock, paper, scissors without XP, not other minigames.

[h2]5. Critters, Pets and Retinue[/h2]
[b]Critter Encounters / Meet[/b] asks about the specific animal: try making friends, catch this one, watch or leave. Collect Always may seek encounters but still asks before each catch. Moved/despawned/reused targets invalidate old answers. Company invites supported real wild species, including both scorpions (one normally, AETHER three), preserving native physics, health and despawning. Temporary company/species memories are [b]not persistent named-animal relationships[/b].

Basic-net capability is built in, not a free net item; lava species need a carried lava-proof net. Gold/statue/released critters are protected. Consent, visibility, tasks and energy matter. [b]A catch is a real item in Pack, not a following pet.[/b] Queued receipts name the item/destination; unstorable drops remain world items without invented substitutes. Catching and witnessed death differ; unknown causes accuse nobody.

[b]Pets:[/b] upper gift row is yours; twelve lower slots are hers. Gift unfavorited reusable items; left-click summons, right-click returns. First gift selects only if empty; later gifts respect dismissal. Icons select, not duplicate; retain the item. One harmless familiar stays separate from your pet: native art/light, custom movement, not original pet AI/modded drawing.

[b]Retinue:[/b] gift reusable summon staves via the staff symbol. Left-click calls, right-click returns. Normal: 1/2/3 distinct types at levels 1/10/20; [b]AETHER all owned distinct types[/b], max 64 slots. No duplicate helpers. Native art/custom combat, not original minion AI or player summon slots. Recall/switch/dismiss/last withdrawal retire effects. Cosmetic flock needs real insects.

[h2]6. Cargo and Character[/h2]
Equipment: 8-12 slots, 99/type. Resources: 60 slots, 50/level/type, max 1,000 at level 20; native stacks apply. Wallet: exact uncapped balance and Here for you! offers. Transfers conserve items; leftovers stay, MP conflicts reject. Tools are capabilities, not armor slots.
