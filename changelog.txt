Soulmates 0.15.1 - Careful Company

- Preserve text entry and open interfaces: wheels and hotkeys wait while chatting, editing names, or using another Soulmates menu.
- Prevent answered multiplayer initiative wheels from reopening before the server acknowledges the choice.
- Report blocked cargo capacity instead of claiming nearby drops are gone. Client-side pickup cannot independently change cargo or world items.
- Synchronize actual companion healing, carried-potion mana recovery, and potion/food buffs to the owning multiplayer client.
- Release invulnerable combat targets. Regression checks cover repeated shots, target-facing, Stay defense, and removal of only the recalled companion's projectiles.
- Stop world work when the matching Sigil is no longer carried. Single-player cursor-held Sigils keep their binding while rearranging inventory.
- Revalidate the carried binding when accepting multiplayer commands, rejecting late requests after a Sigil move or owner death.
- Persist perk-unlock experience immediately and handle a full NPC pool with clear summon feedback.
- Keep all 360 mailbox-note characters, omit exact coordinates from future automatic Field Notes, and reuse feedback buffers instead of allocating them every frame.
- Omit the optional mini-icon to avoid an installed tModLoader packer stream bug; retain the normal mod and Workshop icons and all gameplay graphics.
- Retain existing companions, relationships, forestry, Equipment, Resources, and the uncapped Wallet.

Growing alpha: this patch has isolated engine and static checks, not a new graphical client or live multiplayer playtest. All multiplayer participants must use the same mod version.
