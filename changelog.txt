Soulmates 0.4.1 - Pack and Pathfinder Update

- Added a persistent companion pack with 8 stack slots, expanded to 12 by the Hearth Ribbon.
- Added a visual pack display and Pack conversation category.
- Companions can store the selected hotbar item and unload cargo back to the player.
- Gathering jobs now carry loose items in the companion pack.
- Normal and modded items retain their stack, prefix, and mod data across saves and summons.
- Work can be started repeatedly after each assignment completes.
- Removed random work refusals; mood and energy now determine willingness predictably.
- Added stronger companion light and local world-map discovery while exploring darkness.
