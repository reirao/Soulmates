Soulmates 0.5.1 - English and German Language Update

- Added complete English and German localization for items, companions, interfaces, dialogue, jobs, memories, and status messages.
- Soulmates now follows the language selected in Terraria automatically.
- Localized creator options, Talk Mode categories, companion personalities, talents, forms, essences, and auras.
- Localized the Talk to Companion keybind name and all dynamic Soulbound Sigil tooltips.
- Preserved English as the fallback language for unsupported languages.
