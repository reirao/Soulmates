Soulmates 0.15.0 - Familiar Faces

- Save up to 24 resident relationships per companion, scoped by world, NPC type, and name. Native conversations influence sympathy; first meetings and friendships create memories.
- Add personality-aware native-emote answers, resident-role dialogue, and a Bond conversation for recalling acquaintances. Anger is no longer rewarded as cheering.
- Respect disabled autonomy, interrupt NPC visits for explicit work or combat, and reject replaced NPC targets.
- Anchor initiative answer wheels at the player. The companion shows the work symbol followed by a question mark; the center displays the task.
- Let AETHER use a carried bug net on nearby natural butterflies, fireflies, and lightning bugs during approved forestry. Forestry > Always also permits idle catches.
- Route the exact native catch drop through the existing cargo transaction. Up to six carried insects form a cosmetic native-sprite flock, without creating extra critters, pets, or combat damage.
- Retain 0.14.0 world modes, persistent pointing, independent initiative rules, Equipment, Resources, and the uncapped Wallet.

Living tree-branch pruning is not enabled. Growing alpha: new social visuals, long sessions, full progression, and live multiplayer need playtesting. All multiplayer participants must use the same mod version.
