Soulmates 0.4.0 - Living Companion Update

- Work requests now perform distinct, real jobs.
- Treasure jobs locate nearby chests and report direction and distance.
- Mining jobs carefully extract small batches of stone and early-game ore.
- Gathering jobs retrieve nearby loose items for the player.
- Added persistent job memories and completed-job history.
- Added the Starfinder Bell, Delver Charm, and Hearth Ribbon companion trinkets.
- Resting companions now recover mood and energy over time.
- Companion state is synchronized back to its Soulbound Sigil when work finishes or the companion is recalled.
