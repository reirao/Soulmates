Soulmates 0.13.0 - Growing Cargo

- Separate persistent Equipment, Resources, and Wallet compartments with Terraria item icons.
- Give resources 60 stack slots, paged in groups of 12, and a reserve of 50 units per level and item type: level 10 holds 500 and level 20 holds 1,000.
- Store collected coins as one exact, uncapped wallet balance without consuming cargo slots. Money is collected, not generated.
- Withdraw native copper, silver, gold, or platinum coins. Only money or items actually accepted by the player's inventory leave the companion.
- Migrate existing Sigils and safely return resource excess on summon. Forestry, torches, and recipe suggestions use the new resource compartment.
- Preserve the new cargo and wallet through cloning, save/load, and multiplayer profile synchronization. Multiplayer participants must use the same mod version.

Verification: 4,051 engine/geometry and 2,065 static/math assertions passed. The creator confirmed the new cargo works in play. Broader rendering, long sessions, and live multiplayer still need testing; the 0.12.2 screenshots are not new 0.13.0 captures.
