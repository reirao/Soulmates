Soulmates 0.28.1 - A Little Flock | Beta

- Matching vanilla pet items, including Eucalyptus Sap, stack in the existing companion Pet inventory. Twelve stack slots, 64 total pet items. Gifts and pickup share the limit; excess stays with the source. Modded items retain native stack limits and hooks.
- One harmless familiar per carried pet-item unit, including duplicate types. Select an owned item to call the whole group with that type first. Dismiss rests all; later gifts respect dismissal.
- Separate item/copy identities and spaced, gently animated formation positions. Single-item return retires one familiar; a new carried type leads if the last leading item is returned.
- Compatible legacy duplicates compact without deleting cargo. Saves and transport preserve counts; player transfers remain within native stack limits. Recall, switching, boss mode and owner loss retire bound familiars. Full projectile pools wait without replacing other projectiles.
- Pet UI and diagnostics show stack quantities and actual follower counts; all nine catalogs are updated. Summon-weapon Retinue remains separate and still uses distinct types, not duplicate staff counts.

Installed locally October 12. Development pauses for playtime and feedback, with no promised next release. Physical input, rendered gameplay, connected multiplayer and long-session acceptance remain open. Multiplayer peers need matching versions. Upload, approval and subscriber delivery are separate. This entry contains only 0.28.1 changes.
