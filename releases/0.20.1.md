# Soulmates 0.20.1 - Words in Circles

Local beta candidate, not installed or publicly released.

All 151 native player emotes are now organized by meaning:

- Feelings: joy/affection, sadness/anger, fear/confusion.
- Gestures: kiss, wink and silly face.
- Activities: everyday life, combat and game move/result symbols.
- Items: food, recovery, tools, weapons, materials, valuables and party.
- World: weather, biomes, events and nature.
- People & creatures: townsfolk and creature symbols.
- Notifications: signals, hunger, ailments and bosses.

Companion Bond groups Heart/Laugh into Feelings, Wave/Cheer into Gestures and Comfort/Rest into Care. The actual Rock, paper, scissors minigame remains under Companion > Games.

Folders and leaves share one navigation model. Back returns one level; page arrows stay within the leaf. The category path is shown when not hovering a button. Opening or changing roots clears stale navigation. New labels are present in all nine supported languages and use native Terraria icons.

Native right-click priority, world-target context, initiative answers, automatic abilities, cargo, Sigil persistence and network formats are unchanged. Automated click/geometry evidence is not rendered gameplay; common-resolution screenshots, longer sessions and connected multiplayer remain beta acceptance gates.
