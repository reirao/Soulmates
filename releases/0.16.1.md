# Soulmates 0.16.1 - Here For You

Current Soulmates alpha build, installed for the creator's next playtest after tModLoader was closed. The creator's Steam upload on October 2 at 11:29 CEST is confirmed in the live Workshop changelog; moderator approval is pending. This documentation update does not publish a GitHub release or claim a fresh graphical playtest.

## Changes

- Helpful work now runs before critter visits. Idle decisions are shorter, nearby coins receive extra attention, and already-approved drops within 64 pixels use the existing conservative cargo transaction every half-second during Follow.
- Native item reservations, no-grab delays, capacity, Stay, disabled autonomy and server authority still apply. Choosing Ask or Never does not silently enable the new passive pickup.
- Already-approved Always tasks no longer wait for a free question UI. Ask tasks still wait until an answer wheel can be presented without interfering with inventory/chat or another interface.
- In quiet moments she can ask what kind of company you want. The native-symbol answer fan offers helpful ongoing collection, playful conversation, gentle company or talking later. Responses affect saved voice, mood and bond; only the explicit collection answer grants ongoing gathering permission.
- An unlimited wallet can offer new savings with two lines for each of the five personalities. Choose acceptance, joint saving, playful treasure hunting or deferral. Offers require at least one silver of newly accumulated value and are spaced at least three minutes apart during a summon; they do not mean the wallet is full.
- Only native inventory-accepted coins leave the wallet. A full inventory retains everything; a partial or very large transfer keeps the remainder. Resources/equipment are not unloaded by accepting a coin offer.
- Every personal answer identifies the exact question and bound companion. Expired, duplicate and unrelated answers cannot grant rewards or withdraw coins. Combat, Stay, recall, lost binding and conflicting tasks dismiss questions.
- Forestry sites reject slopes, half-blocks, actuated ground and obstructed growing room. The engine fixture verifies actual native sapling placement and exactly one consumed acorn. Ordinary Terraria saplings occupy one column; the additional adjacent clearance is a conservative growing-room check.
- Optional local Field Notes record question/answer/gift outcomes and current preferences, and explain a rejected Pet Collect mode when no catching tool is carried.

## Scope

Verification: 10,595 native engine assertions and 2,598 static/layout/localization assertions passed. Compilation and packaging completed with zero errors and warnings.

Single-player and server-authority code paths are checked in an isolated native engine. Native UI answer routing and hit geometry are also checked, but there is no new graphical capture or live two-client test of these changes yet. Full progression and long sessions remain alpha test work.

Existing voices and initiative permissions are reused. No new online AI service, automatic world-edit permission, wallet cap, permanent critter-pet slot or living-tree branch pruning is added. English and German Workshop descriptions are maintained separately from the mod package, with current features and explicit alpha test limits.
