# Soulmates 0.28.0 - Careful Finds

Beta release. Installed in the normal client on October 12 after the creator's explicit request, with verified backups of the previous mod and local saves. The existing Workshop item records its upload at 14:18 CEST. Approval and subscriber delivery remain separate; check the loaded version under Mods. No new graphical playtest is claimed.

## One Find Or A Few

Right-click an eligible gem, pot, cobweb or supported decoration and choose Salvage. The companion asks whether to recover just that object or matching finds nearby. Nearby needs another confirmation: their origins are unknown. The permit freezes at most 16 same-type/style objects within 12 tiles; new finds are not silently included.

Supported decorations are coral, books, bottles, water candles, statues, banners and five vanilla painting sizes, not every dungeon or underwater furnishing. Normal native tool progression still applies to embedded gems. Existing Pause, Resume and Abort controls apply.

Observed construction, recognized homes, beds, housing, wiring, paint/coatings, tile entities, neighboring decorations and unsafe supports prevent removal. World generation is not recorded as construction. Construction evidence survives world saves and is intentionally conservative. Old worlds and direct tile-writing mods do not provide a trustworthy universal ownership history: unknown is not natural. Native multi-tile destruction supplies actual drops, and the existing cargo transaction handles pickup.

## Meet The Little Ones

An encounter asks about the specific critter: try making friends, catch this one, observe, or leave alone. Meet is also in its context wheel. Collect Always can seek encounters, but cannot grant capture consent for another animal. Stale or reused NPC identities invalidate old answers; declining defers that individual briefly.

Both native scorpion species can join temporary Company. Meeting uses that existing bounded native behavior and saved species greetings, not a persistent named-animal social simulation. Physics, damage, catching and despawning still apply.

A net catch becomes the native critter item in ordinary Pack, not a following pet. Receipt messages wait behind conversation, include the actual item name and explain the destination. Several catches cannot overwrite one another's receipts. If a native drop cannot be stored it remains a world item; no substitute is manufactured. Gold, statue-spawned and player-released animals remain protected, and lava critters require an eligible carried net.
