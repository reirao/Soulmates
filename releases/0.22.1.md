# Soulmates 0.22.1 - Showcase Repairs

Published beta candidate, not completed beta acceptance. Installed in the normal client on October 6, 2026. The creator reports a good playtest and Workshop publication; Steam records the matching update at 13:08 CEST with moderator approval pending. See [installation and backup verification](https://github.com/reirao/Soulmates/blob/main/tests/CLIENT-INSTALL-0.22.1-2026-10-06.md). Upload, approval and subscriber delivery remain separate checks.

## Included Since 0.20.0

- All 151 native emotes organized into semantic categories; Bond separates Feelings, Gestures and Care, while Games retains Rock, paper, scissors.
- Work Config/Last saves accepted intent per Sigil. Bounded tunnel compass offers cardinal directions/Auto and Short/Until opening. Repeating pointed work selects a fresh target; permissions and protected terrain remain respected.
- Company/Collect share the attention scheduler and approved visits no longer wait behind personal questions. Direct visits work with autonomy off without granting future automatic consent.
- Character view separates Conversation, Equipment and Diagnostics. Native capability displays are not generic armor slots. Explicit read-only export stays local; multiplayer diagnostics labels client state, not server history.
- One item-backed flying familiar: Zephyr Fish or Baby Hornet from a real carried Zephyr Fish/Nectar item. Native animation with custom harmless following, not vanilla player-pet AI. The item remains unconsumed; withdrawing it, dismissing or recalling removes the follower. No ground, light or modded pet support.

## Repaired

- Cargo controls now initialize when the hidden Equipment view opens. Slot withdrawal uses click-event coordinates: right-click for one item, left-click for a native stack. Items that do not fit remain in storage.
- Unanswered personal/work questions hold speech above the fade threshold. Resolution or timeout restores normal fading, retaining the companion anchor and shadow trail.
- Soulcore registers the native eight-frame Fallen Star animation instead of drawing the complete vertical strip as an icon.
- Speech tries the other side or a free position around the enabled corner minimap. If no valid space exists, normal viewport clamping remains the fallback; overlay/fullscreen maps are unchanged.
- Diagnostics separates Terraria and actual tModLoader versions, identifies specific native critter rejection/range/replaced-object conditions and exposes bounded native eligibility details. This changes diagnosis, not permission or creature protection.

## Evidence And Limits

The [real 0.22.0 showcase](https://github.com/reirao/Soulmates/blob/main/tests/LIVE-SHOWCASE-0.22.0-2026-10-05.md) produced [20 unaltered screenshots](https://github.com/reirao/Soulmates/blob/main/media/current/showcase-0.22.0/README.md) and the repair findings. They are not new 0.22.1 pictures. The [repair report](https://github.com/reirao/Soulmates/blob/main/tests/LIVE-SHOWCASE-REPAIRS-0.22.1-2026-10-05.md) preserves the failing baseline and counterchecks. The release has 90,483 native assertions, 174 independent expectations and 54,125 static/controller/localization assertions; production compilers reported zero warnings/errors. These are bounded checks, not connected multiplayer certification.

No new schema or abilities in the 0.22.1 patch itself; it retains the 0.21.0 work/pet profile extension. Legacy Sigils load safely; mixed-version multiplayer is unsupported. Keep all peers on **0.22.1**. Completed live critter following/catching/loss, own-pet summoning, full progression, endurance and connected multiplayer remain separate acceptance work. The creator's positive playtest is encouraging, not exhaustive certification.
