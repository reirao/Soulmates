# Soulmates 0.16.0 - Little Company

Prepared local alpha candidate, not published to Steam or GitHub.

## Critter Modes

Right-click the companion, open the native bunny-symbol Critters branch and choose:

- **Watch:** notice nearby common creatures with personality-specific speech and
  native emote bubbles; do not catch or tame them. This is the safe legacy default.
- **Company:** invite an existing natural bunny, squirrel, bird, butterfly,
  firefly or lightning bug to follow gently. Ordinary companions invite one;
  AETHER invites up to three. No NPC or item is created by friendship.
- **Pet Collect:** give the companion a bug net through Equipment. Nearby common
  natural critters are caught using native CheckCatchNPC; the exact native drop
  enters the existing cargo transaction only when it fits. Lava catching requires
  an appropriate native net. This explicit mode replaces implicit idle forestry
  catching, while AETHER's carried-insect cosmetic flock remains unchanged.
- **Off:** stop critter visits and release temporary company to its native life.

Work, combat, Stay, social visits, initiative choices, low energy and disabled
autonomy take precedence. Gold creatures, statue spawns and player-released
critters are never automatically recruited or collected.

## Native Boundaries

Company adds a bounded steering nudge after native NPC AI. These are genuine world
critters with their original collision, animation, health, catchability, drops and
despawning. They do not fight, become invulnerable, teleport through terrain or
persist as saved pets across sessions. Recall, excessive distance, disabled
autonomy or a lost companion binding ends their temporary link.

Vanilla vanity-pet projectiles belong to players, not NPCs. This candidate does
not impersonate a Player, add pet-buff slots or repurpose combat pets. Engine
contracts: [CheckCatchNPC](https://docs.tmodloader.net/docs/stable/class_n_p_c.html),
[NPC hooks](https://docs.tmodloader.net/docs/stable/class_global_n_p_c.html), and
[projectile ownership](https://docs.tmodloader.net/docs/stable/class_projectile.html).
