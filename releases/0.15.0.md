# Soulmates 0.15.0 - Familiar Faces

AETHER-EGG Alpha update for tModLoader 1.4.4: familiar residents, clearer initiative choices, and AETHER's little insect company.

## Social Life

Town visits now have a greeting and one response turn. Native emotes influence the companion's sympathy, with personality-aware responses and role-specific dialogue. Relationships distinguish world, NPC type, and resident name; the most recent 24 are saved on the Sigil. New acquaintances and friendships become chronicle memories. NPC conversation does not award experience.

Talk Mode > Bond > **Who have you made friends with here?** cycles residents from the current world. Anger is answered with disagreement or caution, not rewarded as cheering. Explicit work, Stay, defense, disabled autonomy, and changed resident targets interrupt a visit.

## Nature Company

Give **AETHER** a bug net through the Pack conversation. Approved forestry can catch nearby natural butterflies, fireflies, or lightning bugs within 96 pixels of her and 320 pixels of the owner, with line of sight and cargo space. **Commands > Initiative Rules > Forestry > Always** also permits idle catches. Player-released and statue-spawned insects are excluded.

Catching uses Terraria's native catch API and hooks. The exact resulting world item goes through the existing cargo transaction, not a second invented item. If another mod changes or blocks the drop, normal world-drop behavior is left intact. Up to six real carried insects form a cosmetic flock using animated native sprites. Withdraw their items to remove it; no additional NPCs, pet slots, or combat damage are generated.

Existing tree shaking, natural fallen-log clearing, and Forester acorn planting remain. **Pruning living tree branches is not enabled**: this request needs separate verification before any live-tree tile can be removed safely.

## Initiative and Controls

The answer wheel opens at the player and displays the task in its center. The companion first shows the task symbol, then a question mark. Answer positions remain fixed while choosing. The 0.14.0 companion/player/world wheels, persistent Look/Gather/Mine/Forest tools, native navigation, fair attention, and individual Ask/Always/Never permissions remain intact.
