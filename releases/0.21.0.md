# Soulmates 0.21.0 - Clear Intentions

Local beta candidate, not installed or published. Includes the preceding semantic emote categories.

- Work > Config unfolds existing work choices, mining modes and ore/material filters. Last remembers accepted explicit work intent per Sigil.
- Area mining repeats its saved approach, direction and stop mode. Pointed work selects a fresh target. Failed orders, Look and settings do not overwrite Last or grant automatic permission.
- Cardinal/Auto tunnel compass: Short is bounded to eight steps; Until opening to 24, reduced by work radius. Cardinal routes stop at unsafe terrain and liquid, also after planning.
- Explicit Company/Collect work with autonomy off. Invited native animals stay invited; ground followers can hop at small obstacles without noclip or teleportation. Modes expose their state and limits.
- Critters > Companion pet selects a real carried Zephyr Fish/Nectar item and summons one harmless animated follower alongside the player's own pet. The item stays in cargo and is not consumed. Dismiss, last-item withdrawal, recall, owner death and companion switching have explicit cleanup.
- The follower uses Terraria's sprite frames with companion-following movement, not vanilla player-anchored pet AI. Only Zephyr Fish and Baby Hornet are supported; light, ground and modded pets are not. Real Company animals, appearance and AETHER's insect flock remain separate.

Existing Sigil tags default to Auto/Until opening, no Last and no selected pet. Work and pet fields extend the network profile, preserving the older prefix; all peers must use 0.21.0. Mixed-version multiplayer is unsupported.

No mini-mods, extra player pet slots, combat swarms or new minigames. [Verification](../tests/CLEAR-INTENTIONS-0.21.0-2026-10-05.md) and [playtest checklist](../tests/PLAYTEST-CLEAR-INTENTIONS-0.21.0-DE.md) distinguish automated evidence from outstanding acceptance.
