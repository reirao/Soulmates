# Soulmates 0.24.0 - Learning Together

Beta candidate for tModLoader 1.4.4, October 9, 2026. The creator reports a
positive first playtest after local installation. This update adds a small
learning layer to existing behavior, not a separate AI engine or free-building
system. Existing Sigils retain their identity, pets, cargo and permissions;
all multiplayer peers need 0.24.0.

## Learn Together

- Observed habits and accepted orders provide bounded contextual hints.
  Actual completed help provides outcome evidence, not invented success.
- The shared attention selector uses those learned preferences when choosing
  among already eligible gathering, mining, forestry, treasure and critter
  opportunities. Existing task executors stay in control.
- After successful help she may ask "Was that helpful?" through the existing
  four-symbol answer wheel: helpful, another idea, less of this, later.
- Answers apply to the captured action and situation, even if the player has
  moved on. Later, silence and player cancellation are neutral; evaluations
  grant no XP and never change automatic permissions.
- Quiet disables evaluations. Defense, Pause/Stay, explicit assignments,
  Ask/Always/Never, cargo limits, tools and progression retain priority.
- Each Sigil remembers its own preferences across save/load. Pending questions
  do not survive recall; stale or duplicate answers cannot reward a later task.
- Construction-item use provides building context and familiar-supply hints.
  It is not proof of successful placement, copied architecture, ownership of a
  build site, invented abilities or autonomous world editing.
- Diagnostics exposes context, intention, attempts and preference values.
  Optional Field Notes record outcomes and evaluations locally, without upload.
- All sixteen new text keys are included in the nine Terraria language catalogs.
