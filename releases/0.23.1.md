# Soulmates 0.23.1 - Pets Within Reach

Local usability candidate prepared and [installed in the normal client](../tests/PET-ACCESS-0.23.1-2026-10-09.md) October 9, 2026. Not publicly uploaded in this pass. File installation is verified; startup, graphical and connected multiplayer acceptance remain separate.

## Pet Management

Right-click the companion and choose the Carrot icon (Pets), or open her character window and choose its Pets tab. Both routes open the same manager. Equipment's pet-inventory shortcut remains available, as do Critters > Companion pet's owned-item choices.

The upper section shows reusable pet-summoning items in the player's ordinary inventory, including non-hotbar slots. Click one to give the real item. The book pages larger collections; favorited items are excluded. The lower section shows her twelve owned pet slots: right-click to summon, left-click to take back. The star dismisses her active familiar without consuming its item. Full storage or a full return inventory retains the item safely.

Correction, October 9: the player reports V still does not open the window in the real client. The isolated tests injected the registered trigger; they did not establish a physical key assignment or graphical acceptance. Use the companion wheel's Details entry. The optional shortcut remains configurable under Controls; no customized or unbound key is reset.

## Boundaries

Giving uses the existing insertion and owner-inventory transaction, with source slot/data, active Sigil and pending-transfer checks. Stale, repeated, full or conflicting requests do not create items; rejected receipts restore companion storage. No new profile/save field is introduced beyond 0.23.0; all multiplayer peers should use 0.23.1 for the new request.

The [0.23.0 pet limits](0.23.0.md) remain: native sprite frames and basic light with harmless custom floating following, not original player-pet AI or special utilities. Ore shortcuts, critter behavior and automatic permissions are unchanged.

Verification uses isolated engine and native UI-event checks. This is not a graphical playtest, new screenshot set or connected multiplayer certification.
