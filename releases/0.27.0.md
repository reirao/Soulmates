## YOUR COMPANION CAN BECOME THE BOSS

The same companion, with the same memories and inventory. EVIL on > Games > secret eye > confirm. Real damage, three phases, no permanent companion death, no loot or XP farming. Stop/Abort ends the challenge; work stays paused until Resume.

The [grouped feature guide](../README.md) covers creation/growth, wheels, work, personality/home, critters/pets/retinue, cargo and local implementation. The sections below contain only this release's changes.

- Open-ended exact experience and levels on the original `12 * (level - 1)^2` curve. Existing Sigils keep earned XP; old save fields migrate. Level display and progress continue beyond 20 without integer overflow or floating-point threshold estimates.
- Level-20 resource reserve (1,000/type) and ordinary retinue limits (three distinct staff types) remain unchanged. AETHER retains all distinct owned staff types, within 64 real slots. Endless levels do not mean endless damage or unlimited inventory.
- EVIL has its own angry-face symbol directly on her wheel. Commands and Details still offer it. The normal persona remains presentation-only, with independent work permissions.
- With EVIL on, Games reveals the secret eye. First click opens a warning; a second click starts a real boss encounter with the same companion. She grows, displays HP, pauses before dashes and fires wider patterns over three health phases. Two seconds of preparation; real character damage.
- No loot or XP rewards. The companion cannot die permanently in the duel. Identity, pets/staves, cargo, wallet, memories and permission choices are preserved. Her pets and retinue rest during it. Other players, residents and critters are not attack targets; other companions do not join.
- Abort, the Games stop symbol, EVIL off or recall ends the challenge. Owner death/loss, distance, five-minute timeout and another active world boss also end it. All challenge projectiles retire; debuffs clear; work remains paused until Resume. Reopening the menu clears an unconfirmed warning. Boss state is runtime-only, not saved in Sigils.

## Try It

1. Check 0.27.0 under Mods. Keep the active Sigil in your inventory.
2. Right-click her and toggle the angry EVIL face.
3. Reopen her wheel, select Games, click the secret eye, read the warning and click again.
4. Watch the two-second opening, HP and dash warning. Fight with your weapons or your own summons.
5. Reopen Games and use Stop, or Commands > Abort. Confirm she is friendly again and her inventory is intact. Resume her work explicitly.
6. Repeat, win, recall during a duel, and verify after resummoning. Report feel, balance or any stuck effects.
