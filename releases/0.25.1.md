# Soulmates 0.25.1 - Home Within Reach

Beta for tModLoader 1.4.4. The creator reports that installed 0.25.1 works in
their latest test and requests publication. This release includes the local
0.25.0 work and its Home/compass correction; the preceding public version was
0.24.0. Existing Sigils retain identity, learning, permissions, cargo and pets.
All multiplayer peers need 0.25.1.

## A Place to Belong

- Bounded home memory belongs to one Sigil and world. Enclosed furnished rooms,
  an actual valid selected bed and resident home anchors provide distinct
  evidence. Separated returns establish familiarity, never ownership.
- **Home** is a permanent entry on her wheel, with Terraria's native house icon.
  Discuss a valid shelter immediately or reclassify an existing home: our home,
  her little home, just a stop, or Later. Stay, autonomy off and Quiet do not
  block this explicit configuration conversation.
- Respect ongoing work, resident housing and unanswered questions. Replies
  revalidate captured rooms/worlds, grant no rewards or automatic permissions,
  and leave the role unchanged on Later. Automatic offers retain their cadence.
- Recognized home storage is not automatically rediscovered as treasure.
  Explicit treasure search remains. This is conservative place recognition,
  not teleportation, architecture copying or autonomous building.

## Optional EVIL

Off by default, saved per Sigil. Toggle in Commands or the fourth task button
in Details (laughing face). Safe idle banter, celebrations of actual completed
work and theatrical Rock, paper, scissors quips add character without changing
consent, task choice, rewards, cargo, damage, terrain or critter empathy.
Higher-priority attention comes first.

## Focused Repairs

- Protect native input, typing, inventory, dialogue, cursor-held items and
  alternate item actions. Reject stale captured companion/Sigil targets/replies.
- Fade answer symbols at fixed clickable positions. Separate the eleven
  companion root hit areas and expose the Home click-to-answer route.
- Improve mood/energy recovery, live-target checks and bounded work timeouts;
  real threats take priority over training dummies.
- Validate live critter identity and companion-familiar binding. Keep Company,
  Collect, twelve real-item pet slots and player-pet coexistence within their
  existing consent, protection and capacity rules.
- Preserve consecutive multiplayer mining-compass choices while replies are
  pending, without optimistic changes to authoritative companion state.
- Complete 997 text keys in all nine Terraria languages. AI-authored translations
  need native-speaker feedback; some MP replies use server language.
