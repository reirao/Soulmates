# Soulmates 0.26.0 - Little Company

Beta release. The creator reports a positive playtest of the installed 0.26.0 candidate. Upload, Steam approval and subscriber delivery remain separate checks; check the actual loaded version under Mods.

## Small personal undertakings

Safe idle moments can become brief visits to a classified nearby home, company for her actual equipped familiar, or a reflection on a carried building supply observed in use. A visit must be completed and she must return before it becomes a memory. Changed targets, explicit work, danger, Stay, Pause and unanswered prompts interrupt it. No item consumption, XP farming, terrain edits or autonomous building are introduced.

The existing evaluation answers shape bounded preferences per Sigil. Later, silence and cancellation are neutral. Existing story conversations can recall the last completed undertaking; Diagnostics reports the active plan, cooldowns and preferences. Save/clone/transport retain personal data, not a stale active world task.

## Her own retinue

Open Pets from her wheel or Details, then use the staff symbol to switch to Retinue. Give an unfavorited reusable summon staff from your real inventory. The first explicit gift selects only when this inventory is empty. Later gifts preserve dismissal. Left-click an owned staff to call company; right-click returns its real item. The dismiss symbol rests the company without consuming equipment.

The upper gift row shows eligible items still owned by the player; the lower inventory holds the companion's real items. Wheel icons are selection shortcuts, not extra copies. Items must remain with her to power a familiar or helper. One vanity/light familiar remains separate from her combat retinue.

Normal companions select one distinct staff type initially, two at level 10 and three at level 20. AETHER selects all distinct owned staff types simultaneously, within 64 physical item slots. Duplicate staves do not create duplicate helpers. Existing staves in cargo migrate without granting permission to summon.

Helpers use the staff's native projectile artwork with companion-controlled melee, ranged or sentry behavior and bounded damage. They defend the same nearby threats as their companion and cannot target harmless critters. They are not original player-minion AI: native special abilities, segmented drawing and arbitrary modded rendering are not guaranteed. Player buffs, pets and minion/sentry slots remain untouched. No fake player or global player swapping is used. Pending inventory transfers suspend combat; recall, switching, owner loss, dismissal and final withdrawal retire bound effects.
