# Soulmates 0.15.3 - Bound Delivery

Uploaded by the creator on 2026-10-02. The latest Steam change entry was corrected
in place from its stale 0.15.1 text; earlier update entries were not rewritten.

Changes since Workshop 0.15.1:

- Fix Host & Play reporting successful creation without delivering the purple
  Soulbound Sigil to the owner's visible inventory.
- Deliver only changed inventory slots for creation, cargo and wallet transfers,
  cargo reconciliation and recall, preserving item favorites and ModItem data.
- Fix cursor-target conversion with UI scale and world zoom.
- Open cargo on a populated compartment and keep storage-tab hover labels clear
  of its item slots.
- Notice nearby resident emotes outside visits and answer according to personality,
  with work, danger, Stay and social cooldowns retaining priority.

The old package reproduced five creation failures in serialized server/client
checks; 0.15.3 passes 10,118 isolated engine assertions and 2,366 static/math
assertions. The creator confirmed the hotfix feels good in play. This is not a
claim of full progression, long-session or complete live multiplayer coverage.
See the [hotfix report](../tests/HOTFIX-2026-10-02.md). All multiplayer participants
must use the same version. New Critter modes are not included in this hotfix.
