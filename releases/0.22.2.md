# Soulmates 0.22.2 - Living Critters

Beta candidate. Installed in the normal client on October 8, 2026; the creator confirms a successful in-game playtest on October 9 and has uploaded it to the existing Workshop item. Steam records the update at **08:05 CEST**, with review pending at the publication check. Upload, approval and subscriber delivery remain separate. See [installation and backup verification](https://github.com/reirao/Soulmates/blob/main/tests/CLIENT-INSTALL-0.22.2-2026-10-08.md).

## What Changed

- Fix the root critter failure: native spawn immunity temporarily changes `friendly`. Ordinary harmless catchable animals remain eligible after that protection expires; greetings, Company, catching and witnessed-death reactions no longer rely on the temporary flag.
- Keep sightings valid at ground level using native visibility checks. Solid walls still block actions.
- Align visit completion with invitation/catch reach and adapt approach speed to moving animals. Real followers catch up sooner while retaining native AI, collision, health and despawning. No spawned copies, teleporting or noclip.
- Open critter/drop context over cuttable foliage while preserving native furniture and placed-torch priority.
- Give a visible localized explanation when a companion-pet item is missing. Her two supported pets remain Zephyr Fish and Baby Hornet from real carried Zephyr Fish/Nectar items. The item is not consumed, and your own pet/buffs remain separate.

Existing Ask/Always/Never rules, Pause/Resume/Abort, cargo limits and Sigil/network formats are unchanged. Ordinary critter catching already includes a basic net capability; lava animals need a carried lava-proof upgrade. Gold, statue-spawned and released animals stay protected. Full permission and pet instructions are in the [README](https://github.com/reirao/Soulmates#readme).

## Evidence And Limits

The [repair report](https://github.com/reirao/Soulmates/blob/main/tests/LIVING-CRITTERS-0.22.2-2026-10-08.md) preserves the root cause, failing baseline and seven focused playtest steps. Two repeated isolated engine runs each passed **90,691 assertions**; the independent audit passed **176 expectations**, and static/controller/localization checks passed **54,126 assertions**. Production compilers reported zero warnings/errors. The broad test-only probe retains 33 known nullable/Hjson warnings.

Normal native NPC/projectile updates cover aged critters, approach/following, direct and consented automatic catching, cargo conservation, queued death reactions, both supported pet lifetimes/animation and recall cleanup. These are controlled fixtures, not a played world or connected multiplayer certification. The creator's positive playtest is separate human evidence, not exhaustive acceptance. Individual loss timing, pet combinations, full progression and endurance remain open. Keep all multiplayer peers on **0.22.2**.

No new screenshots are claimed. The [20 genuine October 5 pictures](https://github.com/reirao/Soulmates/blob/main/media/current/showcase-0.22.0/README.md) remain labeled **0.22.0**; the Workshop gallery is earlier footage.
