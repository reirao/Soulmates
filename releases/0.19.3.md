# Soulmates 0.19.3 - Steady Hands

Growing alpha for tModLoader 1.4.4. Existing Soulbound Sigils remain compatible. All multiplayer participants need the same version.

## Work With a Little More Care

- Protect support blocks touching saplings, plants, trees, furniture and decorations. Recheck planned targets before mining, including direct orders.
- Companion wheel > Work > Mining approach > Automatic mining opens paged native-symbol permissions for ores and observed blocks. Toggle individual types or all available types; save choices per Sigil. Ores start allowed, observed terrain needs opt-in. Tool strength, protected terrain and the selected mining approach still apply.
- Direct assignments bypass only the automatic permission filter, never plant/decor protection. Permission changes can stop an already selected automatic target.
- Settle one energy per eight retrieved stacks, four mined blocks or two successful forestry actions. Reduce work-command and healing costs.
- Recover two energy per two seconds while following and one per three seconds while working. Exhausted automatic or retained work rebuilds a 30-energy reserve before resuming. Combat still pauses recovery.
- Shorten approved-work decisions and revisit delays while preserving Ask/Always/Never, Stay, combat, cargo limits and catching protections.

## Included From 0.19.2

- Player Emotes > Items first opens Food, Recovery, Tools, Weapons, Materials, Valuables and Party. All 151 vanilla emotes remain available with stepwise Back navigation. Ore conversations stay under Companion > Items > Ores.
- Watch, Company and Pet Collect notice nearby natural critters during work. Greeting speech waits behind current lines; stale observations are discarded.
- Give bounded Company visits a turn between completed automatic tasks. Current assignments, combat, Stay, prompts and native catching protections still have priority. Collect still needs a real carried net and cargo capacity.

The earlier 0.19.1 conversation/input hotfix remains included, alongside nine languages, persistent cargo and the uncapped wallet, forestry, rock/paper/scissors and stories from real memories.

## Verification and Remaining Scope

The attached exact tested package passes **37,865 isolated native-engine assertions** and **46,851 static/localization/layout assertions**. Main mod and probe builds reported zero warnings and errors. All nine cultures have 826 keys. Controlled checks include a 96-block assignment, plant/decor protection, rules and recovery synchronization, old-Sigil defaults and production wheel click events at three UI scales.

These are bounded automated fixtures, not independent play sessions or a new graphical client playthrough. Full progression, visual framing, long-session balancing, broad mod interactions and live multiplayer remain playtest work. Some multiplayer replies still use the server's language; AI-authored translations need native-speaker feedback.
