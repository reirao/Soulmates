# Soulmates 0.17.1 - Shared Discoveries

Growing-alpha release, October 2, 2026. Existing Soulbound Sigils remain compatible. English and German are included.

This includes 0.17.0's Terraria, Me and Soulmate mouse modes. Showing a nearby drop or tile also invites a small personal observation: personality and saved voice shape the response, while the concrete target and capability facts remain intact. Direct stays concise. Tired and low-mood companions can share a quiet look without being forced into work.

Coin inspection correctly describes the uncapped wallet. Looking changes no assignment, item, terrain, energy, experience or autonomy permission. Existing native emote reactions and their reward cooldown remain unchanged; this is not a new learning service or automatic task system.

Terraria stays the default for ordinary world and item right-clicks. Right-click yourself or the companion for a Soulwheel, then choose Terraria, Me or Soulmate with the native-symbol selectors. Me points at the captured world target or opens player emotes. Soulmate offers applicable Look, Gather, Mine and Forest tasks. Right-click in an open wheel cycles modes; X or Escape restores normal use. Inventory, text entry, NPC dialogue and cursor-held items keep priority. Changed targets are rejected instead of silently redirecting an order.

This release also brings GitHub source up to date with the earlier delivery fixes, native Critter modes, approved nearby pickup, personal question responses and safe wallet gifts. Companion company guides real natural critters; it does not spawn permanent pets. Wallet coins remain uncapped, and undeliverable cargo stays with the companion. Living tree-branch pruning remains disabled.

Verification: 10,787 isolated native-engine assertions and 3,337 static/localization/layout assertions passed. The graphical client loaded 0.17.1, and the creator played it and approved this release. This is not a full progression, long-session or live multiplayer certification. All multiplayer participants need the same mod version.

The final package was reproduced with the release descriptions and passed the native-engine checks again without gameplay-code changes. The downloadable `Soulmates.tmod` is 1,372,732 bytes; its SHA-256 is recorded in `releases/0.17.1.sha256` and the release's `SHA256SUMS` asset.

Thank you for trying Soulmates. The creator has limited playtime, so concrete reports help: what happened, what you expected, and whether it was solo or multiplayer. We are equally happy to hear that a companion made you smile. Optional Field Notes and the in-game mailbox remain local and never upload data automatically.
