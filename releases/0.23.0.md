# Soulmates 0.23.0 - Pet Inventory

Local beta candidate prepared October 9, 2026, then [installed in the normal client](../tests/CLIENT-INSTALL-0.23.0-2026-10-09.md) at the user's request. Not publicly published. File installation is verified; startup and graphical acceptance remain open.

## Ore Commands

Companion wheel > Work now has four direct entries: Config, Last, Find nearby ore (Spelunker Potion icon), and Mine area (pickaxe emote). Ore search shows mineable nearby ore types and manual pointing. Targets are revalidated when selected. Existing mining modes, progression, plant protection and independent automatic permissions remain unchanged.

## Her Pet Inventory

There are twelve separate pet-item slots, independent of equipment, resources and wallet. Select a real pet item in your hotbar, open Details/V > Equipment and use Store selected item. Recognized reusable summon items enter Pets automatically. Critters > Companion pet offers only the items she actually owns, with paging and an inventory shortcut. No creature is granted for free.

In the Pets cargo tab, right-click an item to summon its familiar and left-click to take the real item back. The active choice is highlighted. Dismiss clears the choice; withdrawing its final item removes its follower immediately. A full player inventory retains the item and active pet. Recall removes the visual while keeping the item and selection on the Sigil.

The loaded engine identifies 78 native reusable vanity/light-pet items in the isolated fixture. An additionally registered mod-item fixture is also discovered without a whitelist. Items require a valid pet buff and projectile, must be nonconsumable and noncombat, and must not summon a hostile projectile, minion or sentry.

The familiar uses the item's projectile sprite frames with Soulmates' own harmless, floating companion-following movement. Light pets provide basic illumination. Ground pets do not gain their original walking AI; complex multipart art, custom draw hooks, dyes, transformations and special utility abilities such as Chester storage are not reproduced. Registered mod-item discovery is not a universal mod-pet compatibility claim. Your own pet slot, buffs and native pet remain untouched.

## Compatibility And Checks

Existing Sigil save tags migrate pet items from cargo without deleting items. The active modded item uses ItemIO identity instead of a raw saved runtime ID. Pet items preserve stack and custom item data in clone, save and profile transport. The new wire field requires every multiplayer peer to run 0.23.0; keep save backups before downgrading.

The focused [test record](../tests/PET-INVENTORY-0.23.0-2026-10-09.md) distinguishes native engine/UI-event checks from graphical play and connected multiplayer. The separate installation receipt verifies the normal-client file. No new screenshot or public upload is claimed.
