# Soulmates 0.19.1 - Clear Choices

Growing alpha for tModLoader 1.4.4. Existing Soulbound Sigils remain compatible. All multiplayer participants need the same version.

## The Hotfix

- Recalculate Talk Mode's native click bounds after category changes.
- Put Items directly on the Companion Soulwheel. Food, Ores, Tools and other topic selections immediately produce matching cargo replies.
- Selecting Me or Soulmate closes the wheel and arms the next world right-click. Keep right-click cycling inside open wheels and X/Escape returning to Terraria.
- Add opt-in local topic, mode and context-action notes without recording raw chat or per-frame mouse movement.

## Included Since 0.17.1

- Real rock, paper, scissors with Terraria's native symbols and independent companion moves. No XP rewards.
- Complete 810-key catalogs for all nine Terraria languages. The seven new translations are AI-authored; native-speaker corrections are welcome.
- Native critter Company guidance, passive Watch, selected Look/Company/Collect context, and personality-aware greetings and death reactions. Catching requires a real carried net and cargo capacity.
- Cargo-topic conversations and short stories from real recorded memories, plus occasional observations of visible surroundings.
- Pause retains work, Resume continues it, and Abort clears it and holds automatic work. Defense remains available.
- Guarded pruning of dry vanilla side branches with a real axe. Automatic pruning requires Forester/AETHER; leafy branches, stems, ground and unsupported tree families remain protected.

## Verification and Open Reports

The tested package passes 36,161 isolated native-engine assertions and 45,957 static/localization/layout assertions. Main mod and probe builds reported zero warnings/errors. These checks cover controlled fixtures and native input/UI events, not complete graphical progression or live multiplayer sessions. See the [UI/context report](https://github.com/reirao/Soulmates/blob/main/tests/UI-CONTEXT-HOTFIX-2026-10-02.md).

The creator uploaded 0.19.1 to the existing Steam Workshop item and is playtesting it. Steam currently shows moderator review pending. Upload, approval and subscriber delivery are separate states.

New player reports remain open in this release: the Player Emotes > Items branch is a flat symbol list, separate from the categorized cargo conversation; automatic Company visits can be overshadowed by other activities. Some server-formatted multiplayer replies still use the server language. Long sessions and broad progression need further testing.

## Feedback and Disclosure

Please report the action, observed result and whether it was single-player or multiplayer. The creator has limited playtime, so practical reports help us improve. We are equally happy to hear that a companion made you smile.

The optional AETHER Mailbox and Field Notes stay local and never upload data. Automatic records omit names, chat and exact coordinates; manually typed notes retain what you write. Soulmates uses extensive AI assistance in code, writing, interface and visual development. The icons and custom Soulkin sprite were AI-generated; other listed native visuals reuse Terraria assets. Gameplay uses local rules, not an online AI service.
