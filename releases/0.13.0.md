# Soulmates 0.13.0 - Growing Cargo

AETHER-EGG Alpha update: separate equipment, growing resource reserves, and an uncapped wallet for collected coins.

## Cargo

- **Equipment:** the existing 8-slot pack, bond upgrades, Hearth Ribbon expansion to 12 slots, and 99-unit reserve per type remain for gear and consumables.
- **Resources:** 60 stack slots, displayed in pages of 12, with a reserve of **50 units per companion level and item type**. Level 1 holds 50, level 10 holds 500, and level 20 holds 1,000. Native stack sizes still apply.
- **Wallet:** collected copper, silver, gold, and platinum share one exact balance with no amount or level cap. Coins never use equipment or resource slots. This is storage, not an unlimited-money cheat.

Open the bag on the Companion Soulwheel. Select the piggy bank, ore, or coin icon to switch compartments. The book pages through resources, with left/right click or the mouse wheel. Left-click an item slot to take a stack, right-click to take one. Wallet icons withdraw the selected denomination, converting from the shared balance. Each withdrawal is limited to a native coin stack and available player inventory space; the remainder stays in the wallet.

Materials, placeable supplies, and ammunition are classified using Terraria item metadata. Weapons, tools, healing items, buff consumables, bait, and accessories stay in Equipment. Forestry supplies and torches now come from Resources, and recipe suggestions inspect both item compartments.

## Compatibility

Existing Sigils retain their identity and progress. Old material stacks move to Resources, and old coin stacks credit the Wallet once. Resource amounts above the companion's level reserve are returned to the owner on summon, or dropped safely when inventory space is unavailable. Saving and reopening do not repeatedly credit legacy coins.

The profile and withdrawal packet formats changed. All multiplayer participants must use the same mod version. Migration is forward-compatible; downgrading to 0.12.2 cannot preserve the new compartments. Back up player saves before testing an alpha update.

These cover all 20 resource tiers, independent compartments, full inventories, atomic world pickup, repeated collection, exact currency conservation, partial withdrawal, legacy migration, clones, save/load, binary transport, enormous balances, resource paging, and production slot geometry. **2,065 static/math assertions** passed separately, including 534 bilingual keys and 293 source references.

MSBuild reports no warnings or errors. The native release compiler reports one existing CS8632 nullable-context warning and no errors. The packager's existing `Image loading failed: unknown image type` warning is unresolved; compilation and isolated engine loading succeed.

The creator confirmed that the new cargo works in play. The automated checks are isolated engine and geometry checks, not a complete visual or multiplayer playtest. Broader client rendering, long sessions, and live multiplayer remain test gaps.
