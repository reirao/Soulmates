## Real Items, Little Friends

Give her matching vanilla pet summon items such as Eucalyptus Sap: they now share a stack in her existing **Pets** inventory. The bag accepts **twelve stacks and 64 total pet items**. Gifts and world pickup use the same policy; excess stays with the owner or world drop. Player inventory limits are unchanged. Modded items retain their native stack limit and stacking hooks rather than being forced together.

Each carried unit can call one harmless familiar. Three Saps mean three Sugar Gliders; additional pet types can follow too. Left-click an owned pet item to call the whole group with that type first in the formation. Right-click returns one real item; its familiar retires. Dismiss rests the whole group without removing items. Later gifts respect dismissal; withdrawing the final leading-type item chooses another carried type while keeping the remaining group.

Familiars have separate item/copy identities, spaced formation positions and gentle phase-shifted movement. They do not use the player's pet buffs or summon slots, damage critters, or multiply real items. Recall, switching, owner loss and boss challenges clean them up. A full world projectile pool waits for a free slot instead of replacing another projectile.

Compatible old duplicate stacks compact without deletion. Save, clone and transport preserve item counts. Returning cargo uses native-sized player transfers, including items whose normal max stack is one. Existing oversized legacy cargo is retained for recovery rather than silently erased; at most 64 familiars activate.
