# Rock, Paper, Scissors - 0.17.2 Local Candidate

## Implemented

- Companion Soulwheel > Bond > Rock, paper, scissors expands three Terraria-native symbols. The center's Back action closes only the game submenu; closing or changing the root clears it.
- Plain native player Rock, Paper, or Scissors emotes also reach the existing observer. Victory-pose symbols and unrelated emotes are not game choices.
- Authority chooses a uniform random move independently of the player's choice. All nine combinations resolve normally. A companion shows her actual move as a native bubble; speech identifies the player's move, her move and the outcome.
- Character replies cover all five personalities in English and German. Direct omits the extra line; low energy or mood uses a quiet reply.
- The server resolves multiplayer rounds. Only the owning client's matching companion identity receives the localized result; invalid choices and wrong-direction packets are rejected.
- A three-second cooldown covers duplicate observations. Binding, an active living owner, proximity and pending-question guards are checked again on authority.
- No profile format or save-data changes. Games alter no XP, bond, mood, energy, cargo, assignment, routine or autonomy permission. They are not a progression reward loop.
