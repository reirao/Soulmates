# Here For You Revision

Build: Soulmates 0.16.1, tModLoader 2026.8.3.0. The original revision was local preparation; installation and Workshop follow-ups are recorded below.

## Real Evidence

The normal client log selected local Soulmates 0.16.0 over the same-version Workshop package. The reported problems are not explained by an older loaded version. The inspected Field Notes include 103 failed planting attempts out of 115, 176 automatic pickups, 488 companion attacks and 36 heals. Those totals establish observed activity, not a complete explanation of the player's complaints or proof of reliable coin collection.

The existing AI checked critter activity before helpful autonomous work. That ordering could postpone gathering. The revision swaps their priority, shortens idle decisions, and adds bounded nearby pickup only when ongoing gathering has already been approved. The shared cargo transaction remains the sole insertion path.

A global question-UI availability check also stopped already-approved Always work. It now gates only Ask opportunities. The engine regression checks that ongoing gathering can start without an available question interface.

The initially proposed two-wide-sapling assumption was corrected against actual native placement: ordinary saplings occupy one column. The second-column check reserves adjacent growing room, while the real placement fixture verifies success and one consumed acorn. The report does not claim this proves every failed planting attempt had the same cause.
